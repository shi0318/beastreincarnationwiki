Players who recommend Beast of Reincarnation keep adding the same advice: **play on a higher difficulty**. Since patch **v1.1.0** (1 October 2026) the game has two new top settings. Here is the full ladder.

## Difficulty modes

| Mode | What it means |
|---|---|
| Story | Wider parry window, for players who want the story |
| Normal | The default; Reincarnation+ (NG+) defaults to Normal |
| Hard | The launch top difficulty |
| **Very Hard** | Added in v1.1.0 – harder than Hard |
| **Ruthless** | Added in v1.1.0 – Emma dies in a single hit; a bonus challenge mode |

You can **switch difficulty at any time** during play, and no achievements are locked behind difficulty. NG+ (Reincarnation+) can be played on Hard, Very Hard or Ruthless.

## What else v1.1.0 changed

- Overhauled attack patterns for some Nushi variants and updated Shidou's patterns.
- Raised bosses and enemies whose difficulty was lower than intended.
- Adjusted ranged weapon damage against bosses (arrows are less of a shortcut).
- Adjusted the power of some Blade Arts Finishers and Bloom Arts.
- Added auto-pickup for drops (Gameplay settings).

## Which to choose

- **First run:** Normal or Hard. Learn the headbutts and red-glow combos first.
- **If combat feels repetitive:** Very Hard – this is the mode reviewers mean when they say "play on the highest difficulty".
- **Ruthless:** only after you know each boss's tells. Use healing blades less and the Purifier Blade's stagger reduction more – being hit at all is fatal.
