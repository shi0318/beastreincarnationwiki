The first shrine in *Beast of Reincarnation* unlocks Steam's **Place of Return** achievement. The official stats page gives that objective but does not publish a count or a location table. The current Steam Community interactive map labels these points as **Wayside Shrines**, which gives you a practical way to filter the region you are clearing without inventing coordinates on this site.

## Shrine checklist

1. Discover the first shrine during normal exploration so **Place of Return** registers.
2. Open the [Steam interactive map](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3778455109) and select **Wayside Shrine** under Points of Interest.
3. Work zone by zone. Do not mark a pin complete until Emma actually interacts with the shrine in your build.
4. If a pin is not present in your version, save a screenshot and compare the map author's update date before assuming the shrine is missing from the game.

The Steam map uses separate filters for **Camp** and **Wayside Shrine**. That distinction matters: a campsite cleanup run is aimed at **Happy Camper**, while the first shrine is tied to **Place of Return**. The [campsite guide](/guides/campsite-locations/) keeps the two checklists separate.

## What this page does not claim

Neither the Steam store listing nor the official achievement page publishes a complete shrine count, universal fast-travel rule, or a guaranteed reward table. This page therefore does not promise that every shrine heals, teleports, or grants a specific item. Those claims need an in-game check or a dated player source for the exact version you are playing.
