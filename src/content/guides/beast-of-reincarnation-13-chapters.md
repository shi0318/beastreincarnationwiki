Beast of Reincarnation has **13 chapters plus an epilogue**, each set in its own region of a blighted Japan. Here is the full list, what happens in each, and how recent patches change a replay.

## Chapter and region list

| # | Chapter | Region | Main boss |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Seedling | Kanto – Ogouchi Buffer Zone | Rangifer (x2) |
| 2 | Creeping Tendrils | Kanto – Ancient Civilization Zone | Lacerta (x2) |
| 3 | The Vessel | Mountainous – The Great Wall Terraces | Nue Malefact + Male Purifier |
| 4 | Stems | Mountainous – Pilgrim's Zone | Taurus (x2) |
| 5 | Branching Paths | Mountainous – Matsukawa Zone | – |
| 6 | Necrosis | Mountainous – Fuji Frontier Zone | – |
| 7 | Root Rot | Drowned – Giant Tree Zone | Corvus (two crows) |

| 9 | Branching Out | Wasteland – Inukami River Zone | – |
| 10 | Malefact | Wasteland – Starfall Tower | Ursa |
| 11 | Budding Realization | Capital – Mount Hiei Zone | Shidou and Nue |
| 12 | The Firmament | Capital – The Capital | Erymanth, Kunai and Kirin |
| 13 | To Bloom, Then Fall | Capital – Center of the Blight | Final boss |
| – | Epilogue: Bearing Fruit | – | – |

## How long it takes

A focused run is about **20 hours**, an average run **25–28 hours**, and a completionist run **30–35 hours**. Collectible-heavy chapters are 1 (18 campsites), 3 (8 shrines, 5 Keeper Golems), 5 (10 documents) and 7 (16 campsites).

## What patches changed for a replay

- **v1.0.11 / v1.0.12:** almost every cutscene can be skipped; finishers can be skipped.
- **v1.1.0:** **Very Hard** and **Ruthless** (one hit kills Emma) modes, plus Hard/Very Hard/Ruthless for New Game+ (Reincarnation+).
- **v1.1.01:** **Hasten to the Capital** lets you skip Chapters 4–6 after the Great Wall Terraces. Achievements from those zones need the Mountain Route on NG+.

There is still no chapter select – you replay the route in order.
