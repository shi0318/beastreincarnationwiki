The story is 13 chapters and an epilogue. That number has been stable since launch. What changed is the cost of walking it again.

Patch 1.0.11 skips more scenes. Patch 1.1.0 adds Very Hard and Nightmare. Together they are the replay patch. They do not add a chapter select, and they do not shorten the walk between Nushi.

## How to use the length

- First run: Normal or Hard. Do not skip a scene until you know which ones are tutorial locks.
- Second run: Very Hard. Skip what 1.0.11 allows. Do not skip a button prompt.
- Nightmare: only after the headbutt and the pack fights are boring. The chapter count does not shrink because you picked a higher label.

People who call the loop repetitive are usually describing the middle chapters, where the region changes slower than the enemy model. That complaint is common in Mixed reviews. A difficulty name does not fix it. Campsite fast travel is the tool you already have. 1.1.0 did not add another one.
