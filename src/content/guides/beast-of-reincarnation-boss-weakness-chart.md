Matching the element is the single biggest damage boost in Beast of Reincarnation boss fights. Here is every major boss with its weakness, recommended level and the blade or ammo that exploits it.

## The chart

| Boss | Chapter | Level | Weakness | Best tools |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Rangifer](/bosses/rangifer/) | 1 | 9 / 12 | Fire (rematch resists Burn) | Fire Arrows, Koo's Higanbana: Seeds, Sunrise Sword later |
| [Lacerta](/bosses/lacerta/) | 2 | 13 / 16 | Shock | Thunderwave Sword, Bolt Shards, Sakura: Javelin |
| [Nue Malefact + Male Purifier](/bosses/nue-malefact/) | 3 | 16–20 | Poison | Hunter's Sword, Poison Arrows |
| [Taurus](/bosses/taurus/) | 4 | ~26 / ~29 | Acid | Acid Arrows, Bottle of Acid, Hazebringer |

| [Final boss](/bosses/final-boss/) | 13 | – | None | Healing stock, parry rhythm |

## Where to get the element tools

- **Fire Arrows** and **Fire Bolts** – early crafting; the **Sunrise Sword** (Flame) is on the path north of Scorched Grassland in Pilgrim's Zone.
- **Shock:** the **Thunderwave Sword** drops from the Elite Golem guarding the Lightning Stone in the Ancient Civilization Zone; Fall Thunder and Winterwake come later.
- **Poison:** the **Hunter's Sword** from the Storyteller Golem beside Great Forest Entrance; **Black Blossom** after Shidou and Nue.
- **Acid:** **Hazebringer** in Matsukawa (below the central pillar door), Acid Arrows and Koo's Bottle of Acid.
- **Explosive:** Explosive Bolts from the Wandering Merchant south of Mountain Plaza: Forefront; the **Explosive Arrow manual** (#6) in the Fuji Frontier Zone. Since v1.1.01 the manual can also be obtained in NG+.

## Tips

- Bosses that resist a status (Rangifer's rematch resists Burn) still take bonus raw elemental damage – keep the element on.
- Duo fights: keep the status ticking on the boss you are not currently fighting.
- Patch v1.1.0 adjusted ranged weapon damage against bosses, so arrows are no longer the main damage plan – use them to apply the element, then parry.
