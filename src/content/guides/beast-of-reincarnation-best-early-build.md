The strongest early setup in **Beast of Reincarnation** is not a damage spreadsheet. It is a forgiving loop: parry ordinary attacks, dodge red attacks, keep one matching Bloom Art ready, and unlock movement before the first difficult boss.

## The four priorities

**First: full dodge roll.** The Tracking Shot route gives Emma the longer roll. It creates room when a parry read is late and is especially helpful against red attacks.

**Second: one reliable Bloom Art.** Pick a Koo art that matches the weakness marker you see on the enemies in your current area. Practise its QTE on a normal enemy before spending it in a boss arena.

**Third: one low-risk blade art.** Start with an art whose input and recovery you understand. More abilities do not help if the entanglement gauge is empty when you need to guard.

**Fourth: Walker capacity and level.** Collect Generator Devices, Electronic Brains and Memory Chips while exploring. Equipment upgrades are gated by the Walker's level, so a damage plan can stall if exploration is ignored.

## How to play the build

Wait for a readable normal attack, parry once, then punish the lowered target. If the enemy flashes red, do not try to force the same answer; dodge, Bind or create height. Save FP for a displayed weakness or a healing QTE instead of casting on cooldown.

This setup works on Story, Normal and Hard because it teaches the same core signals. It is a priority order, not a permanent tier list.
