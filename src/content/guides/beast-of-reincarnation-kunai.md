**Kunai** is a skilled swordswoman heading for the Capital on her own errand. Like Emma she is **Blightborn**, and she is bonded to a Malefact called **Kirin**. Her allegiance stays deliberately unclear for most of the story – the director described her and Shidou as Emma's rivals.

## At a glance

| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Role | Rival, Blightborn |
| Companion | Kirin (Malefact) |
| Fights | Chapter 8, Crater Zone (level 45, scripted loss) and Chapter 12, Capital (level 49) |
| Weakness | Poison (both Kunai and Kirin) |
| Voice | Not published on the [voice-cast page](/guides/voice-cast-and-previews/). |

## Her fights

The **Chapter 8** meeting is a scripted loss that ends after her health drops by roughly a fifth to a third. The **Chapter 12** fight is real: Kunai and Kirin share an encounter with two health bars. Parry Kunai's swordwork, dodge Kirin's one-hit missiles. Full strategy: [Kunai and Kirin boss guide](/bosses/kunai-and-kirin/).

## Rewards

Kirin Memories, **Blade Art: Thunder** and the Bloom Art **Numasugi: Skewer**. Afterwards her blade **[Camellia](/weapons/camellia/)** waits inside the Cleanse Walker with a document.
