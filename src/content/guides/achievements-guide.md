Steam currently lists **46 achievements** for *Beast of Reincarnation*. This is a source-led checklist, not a made-up 100% route: Steam exposes the achievement names and a few explicit objectives, while several story and boss achievements remain hidden. The global percentages below are a snapshot from the Steam achievement page checked on **August 14, 2026** and will change as more players finish the game.

## The six objectives Steam states directly

The official Steam achievement page gives a clear description for these entries:

- **First Fire** — discover your first campsite.
- **Place of Return** — discover your first shrine.
- **Blight-Touched** — enhance an attribute for the first time.
- **Green Thumb** — obtain every type of seedling.
- **Manual Maniac** — obtain the manuals for every arrow and bolt type.
- **Blade Collector** — obtain every blade.
- **Happy Camper** — discover every campsite.
- **Duty Fulfilled** — unlock every achievement.

The store page also confirms that Steam Achievements are included. Use the dedicated [campsite guide](/guides/campsite-locations/) and [seedling guide](/guides/seedlings-crops-guide/) when you are cleaning up exploration objectives.

## Boss and story names to expect

The global stats page names several hidden or combat-linked entries, including **Rangifer's End**, **Lacerta's End**, **Taurus's End**, **Corvus's End**, **Erymanth's End**, **Ursa's End**, **Koo de Grâce**, **Shidou & Nue**, and **Kunai & Kirin**. Their names are real Steam data; the page does not publish a full condition for each one, so this guide does not invent trigger locations or claim that a named achievement is optional.

For a spoiler-light route, finish the main objectives first, then return to the [interactive map guide](/guides/interactive-map-guide/) for missed camps, shrines, and collectibles. Steam Community's current achievement walkthrough is marked **WiP**, so treat its second-playthrough and farming advice as player guidance rather than official completion rules.

## A safe completion order

1. Discover the first campsite and shrine naturally during the opening route.
2. Record every new seedling, blade, arrow manual, and bolt manual instead of relying on memory.
3. Use the map filters only after you know the region or objective you are checking.
4. Leave hidden story and boss achievements until you are comfortable with spoilers.
5. Recheck the Steam stats page after a patch; achievement percentages are live aggregates, not fixed requirements.
