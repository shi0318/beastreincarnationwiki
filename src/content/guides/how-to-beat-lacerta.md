**Lacerta** is the Chapter 2 Nushi, and it is where more players stop than at Rangifer. Steam lists **Lacerta's End** at 43.8%. The full record is the [Lacerta page](/bosses/lacerta/). This page is only the clear order.

It fights with Poison and Slow, and it leaves the water to do it. **Shock** is the weakness: Thunderwave Sword, Koo's Sakura: Javelin, or Bolt Shards. Fire from the Rangifer fight will not do this job.

## Clear order

1. Rest at Reservoir: Interior before the level-13 arena. That is the retry, not the Walker door.
2. Equip Shock before you drop. The [weakness chart](/guides/beast-of-reincarnation-boss-weakness-chart/) is the loadout.
3. Dodge the slip out of the water. A red glow is still a dodge, not a parry. [Red attacks](/guides/beast-of-reincarnation-red-attacks/).
4. The level-16 lair near Nushi's Lair Entrance is two full bars. Do not enter it on the level-13 kit.

Poison and Slow are the status it puts on you. Cure or space the next swing. Do not stand in the puddle and try to out-damage the slow.
