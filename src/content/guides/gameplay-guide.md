**Beast of Reincarnation gameplay** combines real-time sword combat with turn-based decision points. You control Emma directly, command Koo's techniques through the bloom-art menu, and build both characters around parries, skill trees, gear, and spirit stones. This page describes the released game and separates shipped mechanics from details that still need a versioned test. Checked August 12, 2026.

## What type of game is Beast of Reincarnation?

The official Steam description calls Beast of Reincarnation an action RPG with a fusion of real-time and turn-based combat. It is a single-player journey through a post-apocalyptic Japan in the year 4026, with Emma and Koo travelling together. The turn-based part is a decision layer rather than a separate battle screen: time slows while you choose a bloom art, but the encounter is still active.

## Emma and Koo: how the partnership works

Emma handles direct movement, sword attacks, parries, dodges, blade arts, and traversal. Koo is a blighted dog whose bloom arts are selected from a menu and then executed through a timed input. A useful early rhythm is:

1. Read the enemy wind-up and parry a compatible attack.
2. Spend the florescence points (FP) created by parries on the bloom art that matches the enemy's weakness.
3. Use the timed input inside the bloom-art sequence for the stronger result.
4. Return to Emma's sword range while watching the entanglement gauge.

The [first-hours guide](/guides/first-hours-build-and-combat/) covers the early upgrade order. The [bloom arts guide](/guides/koo-bloom-arts-qte-guide/) covers the QTE and resource rules in more detail.

## Parry, guard and dodge

A successful parry is more than a defensive animation. In the shipped combat loop it negates the compatible hit, grants FP for Koo, grants entanglement for Emma, and triggers the current weapon effect. An early defensive input becomes a guard instead, while red attacks require a dodge because they cannot be parried or guarded.

Use the [parry, guard and dodge controls guide](/guides/parry-guard-dodge-controls-guide/) for the input table. The practical lesson is to learn one enemy's readable attack first, then add bloom arts and blade arts after you can identify a successful parry.

## FP and entanglement are different resources

| Resource | Main use | What creates it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FP | Koo's bloom arts | Successful parries and related combat interactions |
| Entanglement gauge | Guarding, Emma's blade arts, Nushi skills and overdrive | Successful parries and the combat loop |

Do not treat the two bars as interchangeable. Saving FP for a weakness-matching bloom art can be more useful than spending it on the first available target, while holding guard for an entire combo drains the resource that Emma needs for her own techniques.

## Skills, gear and spirit stones

The Steam listing describes skill trees, gear, and spirit stones as the systems used to shape Emma and Koo's playstyle. That supports three broad approaches: ranged pressure, stealth-oriented positioning, and more aggressive close combat. It does not by itself prove a universal best build, so this site does not publish a tier list without a reproducible build and patch context.

For a first playthrough, prioritize options that solve a problem you are actually meeting: movement if you are being cornered, a weakness-matching bloom option if a boss resists your current damage, or Walker upgrades if exploration materials are blocking progression. Record the build, difficulty, and patch before comparing results.

## Exploration is part of gameplay progression

The same game loop continues outside combat. Emma's braid provides Lift and Extend traversal tools, while the Cleanse Walker upgrades require materials found during regional exploration. A route that only follows the next objective can therefore leave you underpowered for later encounters. Sweep reachable side paths, campsites, and upgrade materials before forcing a difficult fight.

## What the official source does not confirm

The Steam store description is enough to establish the genre, Emma-and-Koo premise, hybrid combat, skill trees, gear, spirit stones, and single-player scope. It is not enough to establish every damage value, frame window, optimal skill order, or boss tier list. Those details should be tied to the shipped patch and a reproducible test instead of copied from a trailer or a community guess.
