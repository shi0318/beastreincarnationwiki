Steam's global achievement page provides a reliable spoiler-light boss checklist even though it does not publish a complete boss database. The named entries currently include **Rangifer's End, Lacerta's End, Taurus's End, Corvus's End, Erymanth's End, Ursa's End, Koo de Grâce, Shidou & Nue,** and **Kunai & Kirin**. These names are official Steam achievement data; this page does not claim that the list is every field boss or that each name describes a conventional boss fight.

The [Rangifer boss guide](/guides/nushi-boss-fight/) is the only page in this list that currently carries a complete, post-launch fight boundary on this site. For the other names, use the [official Steam stats page](https://steamcommunity.com/stats/2001760/achievements/?l=english) to watch when each achievement appears in your account, then add a route only after checking the shipped encounter.

## Do not confuse boss names with achievement percentages

The Steam stats percentages are global snapshots. They show how many Steam players have unlocked an achievement, not the boss's health, difficulty, recommended level, or whether the achievement is missable. A low percentage may reflect story progress or release timing rather than a secret mechanic.

For combat preparation, keep the [elements and stagger guide](/guides/elements-weakness-stagger-guide/) open. It explains the shipped weakness and stagger vocabulary without pretending that a generic weakness rule replaces a boss-specific test.
