The official Xbox announcement identifies *Beast of Reincarnation* as an **Xbox Play Anywhere** title and also calls it **Handheld Optimized**. The tracked Xbox product facts include Xbox cloud support and Xbox cloud saves. These labels describe Microsoft's platform capabilities; they do not mean that a Steam or PlayStation purchase automatically transfers to Xbox.

## What Play Anywhere means here

For an eligible Xbox purchase, Play Anywhere is the relevant Microsoft program for playing across supported Xbox and Windows storefront versions with the same entitlement. The Xbox announcement is the source for that label; this page does not promise that every PC launcher, Steam save, or third-party key participates.

The current product facts also list **Optimized for Xbox Series X|S**, **4K Ultra HD**, **Handheld optimized**, **Xbox achievements**, **Xbox cloud saves**, and cloud gaming capability. Use the live Xbox listing for your region before purchasing because platform badges and service availability can change.

## Keep store identities separate

- A Steam purchase uses Steam's account, cloud, and achievement systems.
- An Xbox or Microsoft Store purchase uses the Microsoft account and Xbox services.
- A PlayStation purchase uses the PlayStation account and its own save and trophy surface.
