A camera that snaps to Koo while you open a strongbox is not Field of View, and it is not Story mode. v1.1.01 named it. Canceling a strongbox unlock while Koo's Bloom Art was activating locked the camera onto Koo. The same patch marked that lock fixed. If you are still seeing it, check the version before you rewrite your controls.

Beast of Reincarnation has been out since **August 4, 2026**. On **October 26, 2026** the US page was **$59.99**, Mixed, **1,585** English reviews, and **67%** of **101** in 30 days. A small recent month includes people on the patch and people describing August. The chest test takes one minute. The percentage does not open the chest.

## The one-chest test

Update to v1.1.01 or newer. Stand at a strongbox you can already reach. Do not tap a Bloom Art while the unlock plays. Do not cancel the unlock to "see if it still breaks." Let it finish. If the camera stays on Emma, the listed bug is gone on your install. If it snaps to Koo, you are not on that build, or you canceled during the Bloom Art anyway. Read the inputs before you file a new bug as the old one.

Two nearby fixes in the same notes are easy to mix in. Manually canceling Entanglement Overdrive no longer toggles target lock-on. Koo no longer uses items on staggered enemies, and the staggered graphic no longer sticks. If lock-on flips when you cancel Overdrive, that is the Overdrive line, not the chest. If Koo spends a heal on a staggered enemy, that is the item line. Update once. Retest the action you actually canceled.

Field of View is the other camera tool. It widens the fight. It does not stop a scripted lock onto the dog during a canceled chest. Set the slider for combat. Leave it alone while you test the strongbox. One change at a time, or you will not know which fix failed.

## What not to do instead

Do not drop to Story because a chest stole the camera. Story changes how hard a hit is. It does not change the unlock sequence. Do not hasten to the Capital to avoid chests. That skip blocks New Game achievements in three zones. A camera bug is a version check, not a route.

Do not buy a second copy because a borrowed file snapped to Koo. Family Sharing can host the test. Confirm the owner's install is on v1.1.01. A launch build will still show the old cancel. Cloud saves will not patch it.

The Deluxe upgrade does not include a camera. **$9.99** of skins and a named sword will not keep the lens on Emma. Settle the base install. Then open one chest without canceling.

## After the chest behaves

Fight one beast with the slider set, so you know the combat camera and the chest camera are different tools. Parry one swing. If both hold, the August "camera is broken" report is an old build plus two named bugs, not a reason to ignore a Mixed page you have not tested. If the chest still locks on the current patch when you do not cancel, that is a new report. The closing update note invited tickets. It did not promise a weekly camera pass after the cycle it described as ending.

**46** achievements are not locked behind a perfect chest camera. Early pops are common. A stuck lens at a strongbox only wastes the minute you spend arguing with it. Update, do not cancel, open the chest, leave. The Mixed label on **1,585** English reviews will still be there. The dog will be in the fight, which is where he belongs, not glued to the unlock animation.
