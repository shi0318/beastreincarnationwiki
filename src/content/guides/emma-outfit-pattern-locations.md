The October 25 update (v1.1.02) gave Emma something new to collect: alternate outfit patterns. The patch notes say Emma's outfit can now be changed at Campsites and through the Bed Menu inside the Cleanse Walker, and that outfit pattern variations have been placed throughout each region. What the notes do not say is where those variations are. Within a day, a Steam discussion titled "Anyone found out where the new outfits are at?" became the place players compared finds. This guide collects those reported locations, explains how the system works, and sets realistic expectations about what you are actually collecting.

## What the update actually added

Fictions and Game Freak's v1.1.02 notes describe three connected changes. First, Emma can change outfits at any Campsite and from the Bed Menu in the Cleanse Walker. Second, outfit pattern variations are placed in each region, and once you pick one up during exploration you can change into it. Third, starting New Game+ now carries over equipment, outfits and skins.

The wording "each region" is the most useful detail for planning. It tells you the variations are spread across the map rather than sold, unlocked by a menu or granted by a single quest. It does not tell you how many exist per region, whether some regions have more than one, or whether any are tied to a specific chapter. This site has not verified a complete count, so treat any list you see, including the one below, as a set of starting points rather than a checklist with a guaranteed total.

## Set expectations: these are recolors

Before you plan a full map sweep, know what players found. The first two replies in the Steam thread, both posted on October 26, described the new outfits as recolors of Emma's basic gear rather than new designs. One player called "outfits" a generous word for them; another agreed it was recolors so far, while adding that it was still better than nothing.

There is also nothing in the patch notes suggesting the variations change stats. The notes describe them purely as something you can change into. Treat them as cosmetic unlocks. That matters for route planning: an outfit is a good reason to explore a corner you skipped, but it is not a reason to leave a fight half-prepared or to cross a dangerous zone you have not secured yet. One October 31 Steam review summed up a common reaction, listing "there should have been more costumes for Emma" among its negatives, so if you were hoping for a full wardrobe, adjust expectations before you start.

## Locations reported by players

Everything in this section comes from replies in the Steam discussion thread on October 26, the day after the update. These are player reports, not official coordinates, and this site has not walked each one. Use them to narrow your search, then confirm in-game that a pickup is actually there.

**First region.** One player reported two finds in the first map: one at the end of Subcity 02, and one at the top right of the map, next to the area with the smoke signal. If you remember the crank outside Subcity 02, note that v1.1.02 also fixed an issue preventing interaction with that crank, so an area that was blocked for you before the update may now open normally.

**Wasteland region.** Another player reported the Wasteland variation by teleporting to the camp described as being "in front of the lab" and climbing the structure in the lake. This is a good example of why traversal matters for collectibles in this game: Emma's Lift and Extend abilities are how you reach high structures, and a pickup placed on top of something is easy to walk past at ground level.

**Later zones.** A third player posted directional pointers for four later zones, written relative to named locations on the map:

- Ancient Civilisation Zone: north of Abandoned Cliff Village: Entrance.
- The Great Wall Terraces: south of Mountain Overlook.
- Pilgrim's Zone: south-east of Belfry Interior.
- Matsukawa Zone: north-east of Mausoleum.

Those pointers are short and do not describe the climb or path to each spot, so treat them as a direction to search from rather than a precise marker. Fast travel to the named location first, orient yourself on the map, then sweep the stated direction while checking ledges and rooftops.

## How to change outfits once you have one

When you pick up a new variation, the change does not happen automatically. Rest at a Campsite or use the Bed Menu in the Cleanse Walker, and look for the outfit option there. If you collected something and do not see it in the list, check that you actually received a pickup notification. The v1.1.02 notes also fixed item and weapon acquisition pop-ups that failed to display correctly when certain events were skipped, so on an older build or an unusual sequence, a missing notification is not proof that nothing was collected. Open the outfit menu and look before you assume the pickup failed.

Because changes happen at Campsites, a practical loop is to search one zone, rest at its Campsite, check the outfit list, and only then move on. That way you confirm each find region by region rather than discovering at the end that one of them never registered.

## The Pilgrim's and Matsukawa catch

Two of the reported zones sit on the route that the v1.1.01 update made optional. After the Great Wall Terraces Zone, you can choose "Hasten to the Capital" and bypass Pilgrim's Zone, Matsukawa Colony Zone and Fuji Frontier Zone. If you take that option, you will not pass through two of the zones where players reported outfit variations during that run. The patch notes also warn that achievements in those three zones cannot be earned on a New Game run if you hasten, though they can still be unlocked on New Game+ by selecting the Mountain Route.

If collecting every variation matters to you, play the Mountain Route at least once. The [Hasten to the Capital guide](/guides/beast-of-reincarnation-hasten-to-the-capital/) explains that decision in more detail.

## New Game+ carryover

The October update added support for carrying over equipment, outfits and skins when starting New Game+. In practice that means an outfit variation you collect in your current run is meant to follow you into the next one, so there is no need to rush collection before finishing the story. A player in the "This game is WAY better than mixed Steam reviews suggest" thread also noted on October 27 that nothing is missable and collectibles can be mopped up post-game before starting NG+, which fits this plan.

Still, verify after you start. Open the outfit menu early in the new file and confirm that your variations are present before you assume they all carried. The [New Game+ guide](/guides/new-game-plus-guide/) and the [Growth Boost and NG+ preparation route](/guides/growth-boost-outfits-ng-plus-route/) cover the rest of the carryover checklist.

## A sensible collection route

Start with the region you are in. Use the [campsite locations guide](/guides/campsite-locations/) to anchor each search to a rest point, search the reported direction, rest and check the menu, then move to the next reported zone. Leave zones you have not reached in the story for later instead of forcing an early trip. If a reported spot turns up nothing, note it and move on; player reports are made in good faith, but descriptions written from memory can be off by a ledge or a building.

Nothing in the patch notes suggests these variations change how Emma fights, so collect them when it suits your route. For a broader view of how the map fits together, the [map and regions guide](/guides/beast-of-reincarnation-map-regions/) is a good next stop.
