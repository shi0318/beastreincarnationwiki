A lot of recent files sit at 7–15 hours and say the same thing: the story is the reason they stayed, and the fighting only made sense after a few arenas. That is the useful first-hours rule. The hybrid loop — Emma’s blade, Koo’s bloom arts, the parry that feeds both — does not read like a Pokémon menu. It reads like a timing game you have to fail in.

Do not spend the first campsite respeccing into a “best” tree. Spend it on one parry you can repeat, one Koo art that matches the weakness pip, and a campsite before the next Nushi door. Story and Normal exist so you can learn that loop without treating every hit as a wipe.

## What to do before hour ten

- Practise the parry that feeds florescence and entanglement. More blade arts will not help if the gauge is empty.
- Match Koo to the weakness next to the health bar. Do not assign one element as a permanent best choice.
- If Chapter 10 freezes on a cutscene, that is a patch check, not a combat check. See [update 1.1.01](/guides/beast-of-reincarnation-update-1-1-01/).
- If the talky chapters are the actual problem, skip is on 1.0.11 and later. Combat will not shorten the dialogue.
