This **Beast of Reincarnation blighted boar guide** is written for the released game. The useful answer is not a made-up drop table: the public game information does not provide a separate bestiary record for the boar-like fauna shown in the artwork. Instead, use the live combat UI to identify the target's weakness and handle the group safely.

## Read the target before spending Koo's FP

Select one enemy and read the icon beside its HP bar. Then open Koo's Bloom Arts menu and look for the visible **weak** marker. The matching art is the one to test first; the creature's colour or plant growth is not a reliable substitute for the on-screen weakness indicator.

The released combat rules give you four damage-over-time elements — poison, acid, flame and lightning — plus **Destructive** utility for shields and **Bind** utility for movement control. Those are system rules, not proof that every boar-like enemy is weak to flame. Confirm the icon on the target in your current encounter.

## How to handle a blighted boar group

Boar-like enemies are most dangerous when Emma is surrounded. Use this order:

1. **Choose one target.** Keep the camera and HP bar on the enemy whose attack is about to land.
2. **Parry ordinary attacks.** A correctly timed parry prevents the hit, fills the yellow stagger bar and feeds Emma's entanglement and Koo's florescence points.
3. **Dodge red attacks.** A red attack is not a guard/parry check. Move shortly before impact, then take the counterattack opening.
4. **Use Bind when the group closes in.** A control opening is more valuable than holding guard until the entanglement gauge is empty.
5. **Execute only ordinary enemies that can be stunned.** Powerful enemies and Nushi use a daze opening instead of the normal execution prompt.

For the exact parry, guard and red-attack distinction, continue with the [Beast of Reincarnation parry and dodge guide](/guides/parry-guard-dodge-controls-guide/).

## Build a repeatable weakness check

When a group includes more than one enemy type, avoid testing all of them at once. Record the selected target, the visible weakness icon, the Bloom Art that displays **weak**, and the yellow-bar movement after the same attack. Repeat the check on the same enemy type after a patch or difficulty change.

This gives you a useful live answer without inventing hidden percentages. A single missed QTE, a mod trigger or another enemy's hit can make an otherwise identical attempt look different.

## What this page does not claim

No fixed boar spawn clock, unique boar name, guaranteed drop, fire multiplier or night-only variant is stated here. If your current build shows a named entry or a drop, record the exact in-game text and version before treating it as a permanent bestiary fact.
