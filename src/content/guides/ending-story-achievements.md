Steam's official global achievement list names several story or ending-linked entries without exposing their descriptions. The current list includes **Life After the End, The End of Reincarnation, Reincarnated, Soul Returned, Behold, the Truth,** and **Duty Fulfilled**. The names are real and useful for spoiler planning; their hidden conditions are not published on the store page, so this article does not turn them into invented ending routes.

## What the official page tells us

The [Steam stats page](https://steamcommunity.com/stats/2001760/achievements/?l=english) is the source for the names and global percentages. It also identifies **Duty Fulfilled** as “Unlock every achievement.” The Steam store description confirms that Emma and Koo travel through post-apocalyptic Japan, battle Bosses, capture their powers, and seek the Beast of Reincarnation, but it does not describe multiple endings or a branching decision system.

That gives a safe spoiler policy:

- Treat the achievement names as story spoilers.
- Do not assume the names represent six separate endings.
- Do not claim a choice, route, or New Game Plus trigger without a checked playthrough.
- Use the [boss achievement roadmap](/guides/boss-achievements-guide/) for combat spoilers and the [full achievement checklist](/guides/achievements-guide/) for non-story cleanup.

## If you are planning a completion run

Keep a backup save before a major story transition, record which hidden achievements appeared, and note the build number. The Steam Community achievement guide is currently marked WiP, so its second-playthrough outline is useful planning evidence but not a final official route.

The separate [New Game Plus guide](/guides/new-game-plus-guide/) keeps that player-reported second-playthrough information away from the official achievement-name checklist.
