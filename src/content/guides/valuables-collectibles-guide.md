The Steam Community map for *Beast of Reincarnation* separates collectibles into filters such as **Valuable, Document, Book, Treasure Box, Locked Treasure**, and region-specific resources. Its FAQ identifies examples of valuable objects that matter to completion or the Cleanse Walker, including **Facility Modules, Electronic Brains, Generator Devices, Fertilized Eggs, and Walker decorations**. The map author also notes that the Ancient Civilization Zone alone has 21 valuables; treat that number as a dated community count, not an official achievement requirement.

## Which collectible filter should you use?

- Use **Valuable** for the map's named valuable-object layer.
- Use **Document** and **Book** when completing lore or information pickups.
- Use **Treasure Box** for ordinary containers and **Locked Treasure** for the separate locked layer.
- Use **Generator Device**, **Electronic Brain**, and **Memory Chip** notes when working on the [Cleanse Walker upgrade route](/guides/cleanse-walker-upgrade-materials-guide/).
- Use **Seedling** filters separately for the [Green Thumb checklist](/guides/seedlings-crops-guide/).

Do one filter at a time and record the region, landmark, and whether the object was actually collected. This avoids counting a map pin, a screenshot, and a picked-up item as three different things.

## Are collectibles missable?

The Steam Community achievement walkthrough says its current player route treats collectibles as recoverable and uses a second playthrough plus post-playthrough farming. That guide is explicitly **WiP**, so this page does not convert the statement into an official no-missables guarantee. If a story transition changes access in your build, keep a backup save before leaving a region.
