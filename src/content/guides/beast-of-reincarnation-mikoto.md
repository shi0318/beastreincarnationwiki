**Mikoto** is met in the **Ancient Civilization Zone** (Chapter 2), where players first see him fighting an Agile Golem after the first Lacerta battle. He matters for two reasons: he is the reported way to reset spent Amber, and he is central to how the story ends.

## At a glance

| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| First met | Ancient Civilization Zone, after the first Lacerta fight |
| Gameplay role | Amber / skill reset |
| Voice | Not published on the [voice-cast page](/guides/voice-cast-and-previews/). |

## Resetting skills

Mikoto is the reported reset point for Amber spent on skills. Patch 1.0.11 also made skills tied to the flower nodes of the skill tree resettable, so you can rework Koo's Bloom Arts without restarting. If you are respeccing for a specific boss, check the [boss weakness chart](/guides/beast-of-reincarnation-boss-weakness-chart/) first.

## Story role (spoiler)

Player guides to Chapter 13 identify the final boss as a transformed Mikoto, fought in the Center of the Blight. The fight has no elemental weakness and opens with a regenerating Nanite Shield – see the [final boss guide](/bosses/final-boss/).
