## August 17 Steam snapshot

Steam's public review-summary endpoint returned **5,162 total reviews** when checked on **August 17, 2026**: **2,981 positive** and **2,181 negative**, which Steam labelled **Mixed**. These numbers are time-specific snapshots, not lifetime claims, forecasts, or a substitute for the live Steam store page.

The individual reviews in that sample reinforce the same split described below. Some players praise the parry-focused combat, Emma and Koo's partnership, music, and visual style. Others report dissatisfaction with exploration rewards, enemy or boss variety, camera and UI choices, and PC presentation. These are player views, not verified developer bug lists; one review is not proof that every system or configuration behaves that way.

## Check technical problems in this order

Do these steps one at a time so you know which change helped:

1. **Confirm the build.** The official launch post identified version **1.0.6**. Restart Steam and let the game finish updating before troubleshooting an older executable.
2. **Set the actual output resolution.** Check Windows display resolution, the in-game resolution, fullscreen or borderless mode, and render scale. A game can look blurry while reporting a healthy frame rate if it is rendering below the monitor's native resolution.
3. **Test upscaling and image effects.** Toggle upscaling, sharpening, motion blur, film grain, and depth of field separately. Take the same screenshot in a quiet scene after each change.
4. **Use a reproducible performance test.** Walk through the same open area, then repeat during a machine swarm or a bloom-art effect. Record resolution, preset, upscaler, average frame rate, and the one-percent-low feel instead of comparing only a menu number.
5. **Check the input path.** If the button prompts do not match the controller, disconnect other pads, test Steam Input on and off, and relaunch after choosing one layout. Do not judge parry timing while the prompt itself is wrong.
6. **Report a repeatable issue.** Include GPU, CPU, RAM, driver, resolution, display mode, input device, exact location, and whether a restart reproduces it. The developer asked players to use the pinned official Steam forum threads so reports stay together.

The existing [PC performance settings page](/guides/pc-performance-settings-fixes/) has the longer settings checklist and hardware context. Use it after the six-step baseline above rather than applying every tweak at once.

## What the reviews are actually praising

The launch discussion repeatedly points to three parts of the design:

- **Koo is mechanically meaningful.** Bloom arts have timed inputs, resource costs, and interactions with Emma's health and entanglement gauge.
- **The combat has a distinct rhythm.** A successful parry feeds more than defense, while a red enemy flash asks for a dodge instead.
- **The world rewards vertical exploration.** Lift and Extend turn the braid into a traversal and ambush tool, not only a visual motif.

These are not arguments that every player will like the game. They identify the parts worth testing before you dismiss it. Give the first hours enough time to reach the parry, bloom-art, and traversal loops; the opening impression is incomplete if you only swing and guard.

## What the negative reviews are warning about

The negative side of the Steam conversation clusters around:

- soft or visually noisy output on some PC setups;
- performance dips or inconsistent frame pacing in busy scenes;
- controller icon or input confusion;
- a combat feel that some players find less impactful than expected;
- a full-price value question, including uncertainty around Deluxe pre-order content.

The first four can be tested. The last one cannot be patched by a graphics setting. If the setting, companion, and hybrid combat are not appealing after the refund-safe test, waiting for a sale is a rational choice.

## Buy now, wait, or refund?

**Buy now** if you specifically want a single-player action RPG built around Emma and Koo, are comfortable checking PC settings, and are willing to learn a parry-based resource loop.

**Wait** if your decision depends on a polished first-week PC presentation, a broad endgame build meta, or a stable long-term review trend. The Mixed rating is a warning that the audience is not converging on one verdict yet.

**Consider a refund** if you have tested the recommended settings, the input device works, and the core combat still feels wrong for you. A refund is not a bug report; include your reproducible technical information in the official forum separately so the developer can act on it.
