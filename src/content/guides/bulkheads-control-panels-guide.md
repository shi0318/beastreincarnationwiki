The current Steam Community interactive map treats **Bulkheads** and **Control Panels** as separate points of interest. Its FAQ describes a Bulkhead as a sealed gate and a Control Panel as the object used to unlock it. That distinction answers a common exploration problem: if you reach the door first, switch to the Control Panel layer instead of searching the entire region with every filter enabled.

## Gate-first route

1. Note the zone and nearby landmark when you meet a closed Bulkhead.
2. Open the [Steam interactive map](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3778455109) for that zone.
3. Hide collectibles, crops, camps, and enemies, then enable **Bulkhead** and **Control Panel**.
4. Visit the panel marker, activate it in-game, and return to the gate.
5. Confirm that the route opened before marking the pair complete.

The map author also says an Elite Golem often guards the area beyond a Bulkhead. That is a player-guide observation, not a rule published by Game Freak; bring a campsite or a tested combat setup before assuming every opened gate is safe.

For traversal, pair this route with [Lift and Extend](/guides/lift-extend-traversal-guide/). A panel can be nearby but placed above, across a gap, or behind a route that is easier to read after Emma's traversal tools are available.

## What the official store does confirm

Steam officially frames the game around exploration, Emma and Koo's abilities, skill trees, gear, and spirit stones. It does not list a complete gate or panel database. This article therefore documents the player map's filter system and boundaries rather than inventing a universal unlock order.
