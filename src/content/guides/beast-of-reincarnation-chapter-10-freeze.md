A cluster of Steam reviews names the same wall: a **cutscene transition in Chapter 10 freezes**, and the game never reaches the next scene. Combat was not the complaint – the hang was. Here is what has been patched and what to try.

## What the patches fixed

| Patch | Relevant fix |
|---|---|
| v1.0.11 (8 Sept) | More cutscenes skippable; adjusted playback conditions for events |
| v1.0.12 (17 Sept) | Almost all cutscenes skippable; fixed cutscenes not ending properly when skipped immediately; fixed progression blockers in event and boss battles |
| v1.1.01 (9 Oct) | Fixed soft locks from rapidly skipping events; fixed subtitles staying on screen; fixed odd camera angles after skips; spread effect-generation load for performance |

## What to try

1. **Update to v1.1.01** (consoles show it as 01.012.000). Most transition hangs were skip-related soft locks.
2. **Do not mash skip.** Hold the interact button once; rapid skipping was the documented soft-lock trigger.
3. **Reload from the last campsite** rather than the autosave if the freeze repeats – Chapter 10 has five campsites and one shrine.
4. **Verify game files** on PC and update GPU drivers if the freeze still happens after the patch.
5. If nothing works, file a ticket with Fictions support – their v1.1.01 notes ask for remaining issues ahead of the final planned patch (around 19 October).

## About Chapter 10

Chapter 10 (Malefact) takes place around **Starfall Tower** in the Wasteland and ends with **Ursa** at level 46. If you get past the freeze, bring Acid Arrows – see the [Ursa guide](/bosses/ursa/).
