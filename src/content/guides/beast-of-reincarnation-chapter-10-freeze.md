A recent Mixed-score file at about six hours names the wall: **Chapter 10, the cutscene transition freezes**, and the talky pace is what they would skip if the scene would load. Combat was not the complaint. The hang was.

Skip was widened in **1.0.11**. **1.1.01** is the eighth patch since the August 3 launch — consoles show it as 1.012. If the freeze is still on an older build, install those two before you delete the save. A freeze after 1.1.01 is a file problem, not a missing skip button.

## What to try

- Update past 1.0.11 so skip exists, then to 1.1.01 so the transition patch is on the box. See [skip cutscenes](/guides/beast-of-reincarnation-skip-cutscenes/) and [update 1.1.01](/guides/beast-of-reincarnation-update-1-1-01/).
- Do not mash through a tutorial prompt that is also teaching a button. Those scenes still lock.
- There is no chapter select. You still walk the region. The 13-chapter list did not become a menu. See [13 chapters](/guides/beast-of-reincarnation-13-chapters/).

The live PC price is still $59.99. Mixed reviews do not make Chapter 10 optional. They make the freeze the first thing to patch.
