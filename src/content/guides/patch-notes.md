## The short version

Version 1.0.7 adjusts the player camera and text size, limits repeated affinity conversations to once per day, changes the default cutscene setting to Performance, and adds interact-button remapping. It also includes combat balance changes, fixes for saved button-icon preferences, the upscaling reset, V-Sync resolution behavior, a Subcity 01 golem issue, and an interaction problem after cancelling a Bloom Art.

The update is already more specific than a generic statement that the team is "working on fixes." It still does **not** guarantee that every player-reported issue, hardware configuration, or balance concern is resolved.

## Confirmed changes in v1.0.7

The official announcement lists the following changes:

- Various camera adjustments in and out of combat. More camera changes are planned.
- Text-size adjustments throughout the game, with further work planned.
- Some affinity-building conversations that could repeat in sequence are now limited to once per day.
- The default cutscene setting changed from Cinematic to Performance. Cutscenes therefore no longer default to 24 fps, while Cinematic remains available as an option.
- The interact button can now be remapped in Settings.
- The parry window was adjusted when Emma is taking damage.
- Boss balance was adjusted across all difficulty levels, and Normal-difficulty enemy encounters received balance adjustments.
- Button-icon type changes now save correctly.
- **Fixed a bug causing upscaling settings to reset upon restarting the game.**
- Changing V-Sync no longer changes the player's resolution settings.
- The sixth golem in Subcity 01 is easier to find, and Emma should no longer become unable to interact after a Bloom Art is cancelled.

Only the developer's wording establishes these changes. A player may still have a separate graphics, save, input, or performance problem after installing the patch. For a careful before-and-after PC checklist, use the [PC performance and settings guide](/guides/pc-performance-settings-fixes/).

## What to check after updating

1. Restart Steam and let it finish downloading before assessing an old problem.
2. Recheck your selected upscaler and resolution once after the restart. The official fix applies to the reset bug, but it is still useful to record your actual setting before changing other options.
3. If a custom control layout was affected, test the interact binding and button icons in a low-risk area first.
4. When a problem persists, report the exact version shown by your installation, platform, hardware where relevant, and a reproducible route. The [launch support and fixes page](/guides/launch-support-status/) lists the official feedback route.

## What the team says is still planned

The same post says multiple further patches are planned. It specifically names more story-pacing adjustments, further cutscene-experience work, and implementation of **DLSS 4/4.5 (L/M)** and **FSR4**. Those are future plans in the announcement, not features delivered by v1.0.7, and the post gives no release date.

Do not treat an unconfirmed change, a community workaround, or a third-party tool as a future official patch. The separate [community mod support status](/guides/mod-support-status/) explains the difference.
