Parry and guard are the same button. A clean parry is what fills **Flow** for Koo. Holding the button through a red glow does not fill it, and it does not block the glow. The split is on [red attacks](/guides/beast-of-reincarnation-red-attacks/).

Spend Flow on the opening you just created. Then put Emma back in front. Koo is not a second health bar for the rest of the phase.

## A short loop

1. Parry a normal swing. Punish the lowered head if the boss page names one, as Rangifer does.
2. Trigger the Bloom Art or the QTE while Flow is up. Missing the prompt is not a mash you can recover by shooting. [Bloom Arts](/guides/koo-bloom-arts-qte-guide/).
3. If the next tell is red, dodge before you try to chain a second art.

Do not build the whole tree into Koo before Emma can roll. The early dodge still decides whether the Flow window exists.
