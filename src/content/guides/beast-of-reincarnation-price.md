How much is Beast of Reincarnation, and is it worth the price? Here are the current prices and what reviewers say about value.

The game is also on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and it has been offered through Game Pass – check the [Game Pass guide](/guides/game-pass-guide/).

## Reviews and value

On 15 October 2026 Steam shows **1,424 positive of 2,166 English reviews (Mixed, 66%)** and **3,767 of 6,375 across all languages (Mixed, 59%)**. The standard edition is **$59.99**; there are **46 achievements**.

Recurring value lines in reviews: "quite fun to play but not that price" and "I can only recommend this on a sale". Positive reviews that call $59.99 fair usually mention 20+ hours of parry combat, Koo, and the higher difficulty modes.

## What you get for the money

- 13 chapters and an epilogue (about 20–35 hours).
- 21 blades, crossbow and elemental ammo, two skill trees.
- Story, Normal, Hard, Very Hard and Ruthless modes plus NG+.
- Eight free patches so far, with one more planned around 19 October.

## Verdict

Buy at full price if you love parry-heavy action and are happy to play on Hard or Very Hard. Otherwise, a discount makes the Mixed reviews much easier to accept.
