Ogouchi is one of the named regions on the chapter walk, and it plays like the screenshots: river crossings, a ruined span, and packs that show up before the next Nushi. It is not a dungeon with a door. If you are looking for a room label, you will miss the fight that is already on the bank.

## How to cross it

- Clear the thin enemies with Koo or the crossbow before you fight the tall one. The tall one is the parry. The thin ones end the run.
- Campsites are the save, not the story scene. If you walked past a fire because a cutscene was queued, go back. 1.0.11 lets you skip more scenes. It does not move the fire.
- Spirit Stones and seedlings here are collectibles, not combat stats. Grab them on the way back from a boss, not in the middle of a pack.

The full region list is on the [walkthrough](/walkthrough/). Ogouchi is the stretch people get lost in because the bridge reads as a landmark and the path does not follow the bridge.
