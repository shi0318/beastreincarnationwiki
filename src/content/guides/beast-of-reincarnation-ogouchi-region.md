**Ogouchi Buffer Zone** (Kanto Region) is where Beast of Reincarnation begins. Chapter 1, Seedling, teaches traversal, campsites, the parry and Koo – and ends with two Rangifer fights.

## Region facts

| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Chapter | 1 – Seedling |
| Campsites | 18 |
| Wayside shrines | 0 |
| Documents | 4 |
| Keeper Golems | 3 |
| Boss | Rangifer (level 9 crater, level 12 lair) |
| Blades | Cirrus Sword, Mechblade (Light) |

## Route through Ogouchi

1. **Learn the basics** on the riverbanks: parry Heavy Golems, use Koo on the small enemies.
2. **Cliff-Facing Tower** – activate the campsite, then fight Rangifer in the crater lake below (fire weakness).
3. **Subcity 01–03** – underground areas; the final room of Subcity 01 has an Agile Golem (level 7).
4. **Nushi's Lair: Gate Approach** – talk to the Retired Gatekeeper for the **Cirrus Sword** and the Yellow Key cave (Grizzly Malefact, level 10).
5. **Ruined Tower** – climb to the top for the **Mechblade (Light)** in the tent behind the Elite Golem.
6. **Rangifer rematch** past the locked gate near Subcity 03.

## Optional elites

Heavy Golems at Cliffside Village (8), the far north-west rock (6), North Ruined Tower (9), Ruined Tower summit (9), Disposal Site (8) and the Quarry (9). Full list: [elite enemies](/guides/beast-of-reincarnation-elite-golems-locations/).

## Tips

- Light every campsite you pass – 18 is the most in any region and they are your fast-travel network.
- The Amber farm map spots in this zone are good for early levels – see the [Amber farming guide](/guides/beast-of-reincarnation-amber-farming/).
