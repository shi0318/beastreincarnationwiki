Crash and stutter reports have followed Beast of Reincarnation since its 3 August 2026 PC launch. Eight patches later, most of the common causes have a fix. Use this checklist before you assume a corrupt save.

## Current state

On 15 October 2026 Steam shows **1,424 positive of 2,166 English reviews (Mixed, 66%)** and **3,767 of 6,375 across all languages (Mixed, 59%)**. The standard edition is **$59.99**; there are **46 achievements**.

## Fixes by patch

| Patch | Performance and stability |
|---|---|
| v1.0.7 | Cutscenes default to Performance (not 24 fps Cinematic); camera adjustments |
| v1.0.8 | DLSS 4/4.5 and FSR 4 support; camera distance options |
| v1.0.9 | Ultrawide support; DLSS/FSR frame generation; upscaling fix for the Microsoft Store/Xbox PC version |
| v1.0.10 | Film Grain and Chromatic Aberration toggles; post-processing toggle in cutscenes |
| v1.0.12 | Frame generation options adapt to RTX 40/50 GPUs; DLSS-Dynamic frame-rate target on 120 Hz+ monitors |
| v1.1.01 | Effect generation load distributed for better overall performance; environment collision fixes |

## Checklist

1. **Update** to v1.1.01.
2. **Crash on boot:** verify files, update GPU drivers, and disable overlays.
3. **Crash in cutscenes:** set cinematic mode to Performance and avoid rapid skipping (a documented soft-lock cause).
4. **Stutter in combat:** turn off frame generation first, then lower effects – Bloom Arts and Malefact swarms are the heaviest scenes.
5. **Xbox PC app users:** make sure you are past v1.0.9, which fixed missing upscaling options.

## Still crashing?

Note the region, whether Koo's Bloom Arts were active, and your GPU, then file a ticket with Fictions support. The next patch, planned for around 19 October, is the last in the current frequent-update phase.
