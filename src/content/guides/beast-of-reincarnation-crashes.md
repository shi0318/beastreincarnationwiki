Crash reports are still in recent **Beast of Reincarnation** Steam reviews, including one-line “Keeps Crashing for me.” Older launch notes talked about graphics issues that players hoped later patches would fix. The store rating remains **Mixed** (**1,379 / 2,106**).

## What to do first

Use the PC performance and post-launch patch pages before assuming the save is corrupt. If the crash is on boot, verify files and GPU drivers. If it is mid-combat, note the region and whether Koo Bloom Arts were active.

This page does not invent a universal .ini fix.
