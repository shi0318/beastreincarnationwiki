The US Steam page on **October 26, 2026** listed Beast of Reincarnation at **$59.99** with no discount. English reviews were **Mixed**. The page counted **1,585** reviews for the game, and **67%** of **101** reviews in the last 30 days were positive. That is a small recent slice next to a longer Mixed line. It is not a parry timing chart.

A close camera made launch reviews sound like the combat was random. v1.1.01 put Field of View in Options. One fight on Normal, after you move that slider, tells you more than refreshing 101 reviews. If you cannot see the windup, the label is not the problem. The frame is.

## The fight

Confirm the install is v1.1.01 or newer. Stay on Normal. Open Options and pull Field of View until the enemy's windup fits and Emma is still readable. Stop before the parry flash is a speck. Fight one ordinary beast. Parry one readable swing. Dodge one red attack. Stop.

If that parry landed, the recent 67% did not hide a broken combat system from you. You can still dislike the story. You can still refuse **$59.99**. You cannot say you never saw the swing. If the parry missed because the swing started outside the frame, you are not on the patch, or the slider is not far enough. Do not drop to Story to fix a camera. Story changes damage. It does not move the lens.

The skip-prompt toggle is in Settings, not in this fight. Hide it if you watch events. Leave it if you skip them. A subtitle that used to stick after a skip is on the fixed list in the same patch. Do not spend the test hour in a cinematic.

## What the 101 reviews are allowed to decide

They are a month of English reviewers, small enough that a handful of files can move 67%. The longer English line, **1,585** and Mixed, is the stabler fact. Both sit next to full price. Use them to borrow the game if you are unsure. Do not use them instead of the fight.

Family Sharing and Steam Cloud are on the store feature list. One borrowed hour is this test. Do not start a long boss on a borrow if the owner needs the library back. Do not buy the Deluxe add-on to settle a percentage. The base game is the question. Skins are the answer after the question is yes.

Hasten to the Capital is not part of the test. That skip, after the Great Wall Terraces, blocks New Game achievements in Pilgrim's Zone, Matsukawa Colony Zone, and Fuji Frontier Zone. A one-fight check does not need three zones, and it should not burn them.

## After the swing

If you want the file, pay **$59.99** on the account that will keep the save. Cloud follows that account. If you do not want the file, you just saved the price. If you need a sale, the October 26 button did not show one. Waiting for Mixed to become Very Positive is not a plan this page supports. Waiting for a discount is a store decision. Check the button later. Do not check it instead of the parry.

**46** achievements were on the store record the last time that count was checked. Today's page did not ask you to grind them before the first fight. Purifier-style early pops are common. The late ones are not. One visible parry is the gate. The label is the crowd. The crowd cannot see your frame.

67% of 101 recent English reviews can swing. 1,585 Mixed is the longer line on the same $59.99 page. Neither number parries. v1.1.01's field-of-view slider does. Stay on Normal for the test. Dodge one red attack after the parry so you know red is not a parry. Hide the skip prompt only after the fight, if you watch events. Do not buy Deluxe to settle the label. Do not hasten. Do not start a long boss on a borrowed library. One visible swing is the gate. If you want the file after that, pay on the account that keeps the cloud save. If you do not, the Mixed page did its job and the $59.99 stays unspent.
