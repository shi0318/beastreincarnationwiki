**Emma** is the protagonist of Beast of Reincarnation – an outcast who carries the blight inside her and travels west through a ruined Japan without her past memories or emotions. You control her directly while commanding her dog, **Koo**.

## At a glance

| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Role | Playable protagonist, Blightborn |
| Weapons | 21 blades (Mechblades to Kanzashi), crossbow and bows with elemental ammo |
| Movement | Hair-based traversal (Lift and Extend) |
| Japanese voice | Yui Ishikawa, named in the June cast announcement |
| English voice | Not named on the [voice-cast page](/guides/voice-cast-and-previews/). |

## How Emma fights

- **Blades and Blade Arts:** each blade has a passive (Shock, Poison, Acid, Flame, healing). Blade Arts and Finishers are earned from bosses – Blade Art: Dokuzeri (Lacerta), Blade Art: Nue (Shidou and Nue), Blade Art: Thunder (Kunai and Kirin).
- **Parry:** the core verb – parries build Flow for Koo. See the [parry guide](/guides/beast-of-reincarnation-emma-parry-guide/).
- **Ranged:** Spring Thunder Crossbow and bows with Fire, Lightning, Poison, Acid and Explosive ammo for boss weaknesses.
- **Entanglement Overdrive:** a burst state built by parries and dodges, strongest during knockdowns.

## Progression

- **Giant Tree Budburst** achievement: every Emma skill (the full tree is a level-100 goal).
- **Blademaster:** fully upgraded Blade Arts and Finishers – some only open in New Game+.
- Mikoto is the reported Amber reset, and v1.0.11 allowed resetting flower-node skills.
