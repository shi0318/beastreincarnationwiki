**Emma** is the human lead you steer through blighted Japan in **Beast of Reincarnation**. She handles direct swordplay while you command **Koo**. Steam achievements split her progression: **Giant Tree Budburst** is all of Emma's skills; **Blademaster** is fully upgraded Blade Arts and Finishers.

## What is confirmed

The store copy frames a hybrid combat system. The Steam achievement guide used on this site treats level 100 as the full-tree target and reports some Blade Arts unlocking in New Game+. Mikoto in the Ancient Civilization region is the reported reset for Amber.

Reviews that call cutscene characters flat are talking about presentation, not Emma's move list.
