Steam's **Green Thumb** achievement requires obtaining every type of seedling. The official achievement page confirms the objective but does not list every crop. The current Steam Community map guide lists crop and seedling filters that include **Strawberry, Apple, Rice, Soy, Tomato, Potato, Carrot, Lettuce, Wheat, and Life Seedling** entries across its indexed zones. Treat that list as a live player-maintained reference rather than an official final table.

## How to track Green Thumb progress

1. Keep a handwritten or spreadsheet checklist with one row for every seedling type you have actually obtained.
2. On the [Steam Community map](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3778455109), enable one crop or seedling filter at a time.
3. Clear a region's visible seedling markers before moving on, then compare the result with your in-game collection.
4. Do not count a crop shown in a screenshot as collected until the item appears in your save.

The map author says seedlings return to Emma's Cleanse Walker and connect to the Walker's kitchen, where crops are used for cooking recipes and buffs. The exact recipe list, ingredient quantities, and buff durations are not published on the official Steam store page, so this page does not fill those fields with guesses. For upgrade materials, use the separate [Cleanse Walker materials guide](/guides/cleanse-walker-upgrade-materials-guide/).

## Seedlings are different from valuables

The map also has **Valuable**, **Treasure Box**, and **Locked Treasure** filters. Keep those separate from crop entries: the achievement asks for every seedling type, not every collectible on the map. The [interactive map guide](/guides/interactive-map-guide/) explains how to switch filters without turning a cleanup run into an unreadable pin wall.
