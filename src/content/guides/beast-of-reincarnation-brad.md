**Brad** pilots and maintains the walking **Purification Ship** – the Cleanse Walker – that carries Emma and Koo between regions. He is the closest thing Beast of Reincarnation has to a hub character: you return to his ship between zones, and he becomes one of Emma's unlikely allies.

## At a glance

| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Role | Pilot and mechanic of the Cleanse Walker |
| Where | Aboard the Walker between regions |
| Voice | Not assigned on the [voice-cast page](/guides/voice-cast-and-previews/). The June announcement names Akio Otsuka without a character. |

## What Brad does for you

- **Travel:** the Walker moves you between the 13 regions along the story route.
- **Shop:** merchant blades such as the **Bell Sword** and **Winterwake** can be bought from Brad later if you missed the Wandering Merchant.
- **Upgrades:** the Walker is where Cleanse Walker upgrade materials matter – see the [Cleanse Walker upgrade guide](/guides/cleanse-walker-upgrade-materials-guide/).
- **Story:** affinity conversations aboard the Walker were limited to once per day in patch 1.0.7 so they no longer repeat back to back.

## Notable moments

After the Chapter 12 fight against Kunai and Kirin, Kunai's blade **Camellia** and a document are left inside the Walker, opposite Emma's bed. Kagura also travels with you on the Walker for part of the story.
