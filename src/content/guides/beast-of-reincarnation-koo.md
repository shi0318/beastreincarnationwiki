**Koo** is the stray dog who travels with Emma – and, like her, he is touched by the blight; he is himself a Malefact. In battle you command his techniques while controlling Emma directly, the hybrid real-time and command system Game Freak built the game around.

## At a glance

| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Species | Dog (blighted, a Malefact) |
| Role | Companion, commanded in combat |
| Skill achievements | Giant Tree in Bloom (all skills), Floral Profusion (all flower techniques) |
| Co-op finisher achievement | Koo de Grace (25.6% of Steam players) |

## Bloom Arts and Nushi skills

- **Bloom Arts** are elemental and status attacks named after flowers – Higanbana (fire), Sakura (shock javelin), Dokuzeri, Kiku (Bloom Strike), Erica (Strangle), Numasugi (Skewer).
- **Nushi skills** are learned from defeated bosses – Rangifer's restraint, Taurus's Charging Botan, Lacerta's Dokuzeri Shower.
- Both are powered by **Flow**, built by Emma's parries.

## Story notes

Koo is central to the story, including a mandatory late encounter with his own Nushi form. Many Mixed reviews still single out Koo as the best part of the game.

## Cosmetics

Koo has alternative skins (including a Shiba look). Since v1.0.11 skins and cosmetic items are visible during cutscenes.
