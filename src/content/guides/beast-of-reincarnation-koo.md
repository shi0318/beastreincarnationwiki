**Koo** is the blighted dog you command in **Beast of Reincarnation**. Store copy says you command Koo's techniques while Emma fights. Steam splits his completion: **Giant Tree in Bloom** is all Koo skills; **Floral Profusion** is fully upgraded flower techniques.

## Combat role

Bloom Arts are the elemental and status tools. Fire options such as Higanbana: Seeds matter on Rangifer. QTE timing for Bloom Arts has its own page. Reviews that like the game almost always mention the dog; reviews that bounce rarely say Koo is the problem.

**Koo de Grâce** is a Steam achievement at **25.6%**. This site does not treat that name as a conventional boss.
