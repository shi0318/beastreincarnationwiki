This is a status page for facts that Fictions and Game Freak have published after launch. It separates an official statement from a player report, and it does not turn a request for feedback into a promise that a particular feature or performance problem will be changed. Details were checked on **August 10, 2026**.

The short version is straightforward: the official launch announcement identifies **version 1.0.6** as the launch build, official **v1.0.7** patch notes are now published, the reported pre-order Deluxe entitlement issue has an official Steam resolution, and the team directs feedback and technical reports to its pinned Steam discussion threads.

## What the launch announcement confirms

The official [Beast of Reincarnation is Available Now](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2001760/view/1839676055896142) announcement identifies **1.0.6** as the release version. That tells us which build launched; it does not establish that 1.0.6 is the version installed on every PC today. Steam may have delivered another update by the time you read this.

To identify the build on your own installation, let Steam finish updating first, then check the game version shown by the game or its current Steam update state. When you write a technical report, include that observed version rather than assuming the launch number still applies.

The same announcement says feedback and technical issues should go to the pinned threads in the official Steam discussions. Use the official route before copying a report into unrelated community posts, because a useful report needs enough context for the team to reproduce it.

## Official v1.0.7 patch notes are now available

On **August 9, 2026**, Fictions and Game Freak published **v1.0.7**. It includes camera and text-size adjustments, cutscene and control changes, balance changes, several bug fixes, and an official fix for the upscaling settings resetting after a restart. Read the complete, version-specific [official v1.0.7 patch notes](/guides/patch-notes/) before assuming a community report has or has not been addressed.

## The Deluxe pre-order entitlement issue is marked fixed

On 2026-08-06 China time, Fictions and Game Freak published a specific resolution for Deluxe pre-orders whose DLC entitlement did not appear in Steam. Their announcement says Valve added the entitlement to the pre-order Deluxe package, including applicable purchases through Steam and key sites. The first instruction is to **restart Steam**.

That is an official resolution for one narrowly defined ownership issue. It is not evidence that every missing item, cosmetic, save problem, performance issue, or post-launch purchase has the same cause. For the package contents, ownership checks, and the precise boundary of the fix, use the dedicated [Deluxe DLC entitlement guide](/guides/deluxe-dlc-entitlement-fix-guide/).

## A useful report has a reproducible shape

The developer's launch post supplies the destination for feedback; the following structure is our editorial recommendation for making a report actionable:

1. State whether the problem is a crash, display issue, input issue, missing entitlement, progression block, or gameplay concern.
2. Give the game version you actually see, plus platform and region where relevant.
3. For a PC issue, include CPU, GPU, RAM, operating system, driver version, output resolution, display mode, and controller or keyboard setup.
4. List the smallest repeatable route: location, action, result, and whether restarting or reloading changes it.
5. Attach a screenshot, short video, error message, or save-related information only after removing private account or payment details.

This does not guarantee a fix. It makes the difference between "the game is broken" and a report another player or the support team can test. For community-reported graphics and setup checks, see the separate [PC performance and settings guide](/guides/pc-performance-settings-fixes/).

## What has not been officially promised here

The v1.0.7 post gives a detailed change list and says more patches are planned. It does not give a date or complete scope for the next update, and it does not establish a universal fix for every community report. Do not treat a popular discussion thread as a patch note. Compare future claims with the live Steam news page before changing settings or buying DLC.

For a dated read of player sentiment rather than a support statement, see [Steam reception and issues](/guides/steam-reception-and-issues/). For the confirmed PC requirements that the publisher says superseded the earlier erroneous list, see [PC system requirements](/guides/pc-system-requirements/).
