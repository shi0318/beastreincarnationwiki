Game Freak has been unusually clear about *why* Emma and Koo are travelling, and unusually quiet about almost everything else. This page collects the story and setting details that are actually stated in official material — the Steam store description, the Xbox Developer Direct deep dive, and the State of Play release-date announcement — and marks the edges where the confirmed information stops.

## The setting: Year 4026

The world is **post-apocalyptic Japan**, and the official copy gives it a date: **Year 4026**. That is far enough out that nothing about the present day survives intact, which is exactly what the screenshots show — wasteland punctuated by ruins and machinery.

The most distinctive thing Game Freak says about the world is that it is **impermanent**. The official phrasing is a world "where forests suddenly burst forth amidst the wasteland," with ever-changing scenery. Take that literally: the environment is described as something that transforms, not a fixed set of biomes you tick off.

The thing corrupting it all is **the blight**. Official copy calls it a world "ravaged by blight," and it is the connective tissue between the setting, the two protagonists, and the enemies you fight.

## Emma: an outcast, because of what she carries

Emma is described as **an outcast shunned from society for her affliction**. Two things are worth pulling out of that short sentence:

1. Her exclusion is **social**, not circumstantial. People pushed her out.
2. The reason is an **affliction** — Game Freak's word — which sits in the same vocabulary as the blight ravaging the world.

Alongside that, official copy states plainly that **humanity's only hope lies with Emma and Koo**. So the outcast is also the last resort. That tension is the story's premise, and it is the reason the marketing keeps pairing beauty with brutality.

## Koo: the blighted dog

Koo is officially described as **the blighted dog**. Not a pet, not a summon — a second afflicted character. Game Freak's pitch for the whole game is **"one-person, one-dog action RPG,"** and the emotional framing in official copy is specific: you are meant to feel **moments of loneliness**, offset by the **reliability and comfort of your companion**.

The store text also says that as the pair travel, **their bond and abilities blossom** — bond and capability are described together, which lines up with the [progression systems](/guides/progression-and-builds/) and the [hybrid combat](/guides/combat-overview-trailer/) where you issue Koo commands mid-fight.

## The goal: capture Boss powers, reach the Beast

This is the clearest structural statement Game Freak has made about the campaign. Official copy says Emma and Koo **met in the far east**, and are **tasked with battling Bosses from all over the world and capturing their powers in order to defeat the Beast of Reincarnation**.

Three implications, all reasonable to read from that sentence:

- The Bosses are **spread geographically**, and the journey is the structure.
- Boss powers are **taken and kept** — progression is tied to encounters, not just menus.
- The title character is the **endpoint**, and you are expected to arrive underpowered and build toward it.

Our [Nushi boss page](/guides/nushi-boss-fight/) and the [colossal wasteland boss page](/guides/wasteland-colossal-boss/) cover the two Bosses shown in official footage so far.

## "A story filled with secrets"

The narrative framing in official copy is deliberately withholding, and worth quoting because of how strong it is: a **brutal narrative where every character hides a dangerous truth**, and an invitation to **uncover the intertwined fates of Emma and Koo**.

"Intertwined fates" is the line to watch. Emma is afflicted, Koo is blighted, and the game's title is *Beast of Reincarnation*. Game Freak has connected those dots for nobody, and we are not going to pretend otherwise — but they have clearly signposted that the connection is the story.

## Confirmed vs not confirmed

- **Confirmed:** post-apocalyptic Japan in Year 4026; Emma is an outcast shunned for her affliction; Koo is the blighted dog; humanity's hope rests on the pair; they met in the far east; the goal is to beat Bosses worldwide, capture their powers, and defeat the Beast of Reincarnation; the world changes, with forests erupting from wasteland; every character hides a dangerous truth.
- **Not confirmed:** what Emma's affliction actually is; what the blight is or where it came from; what the Beast of Reincarnation is; how Emma and Koo met; whether Koo was human; the ending, and whether there is more than one. None of this has been officially stated.

Anything you read that answers those questions before **4 August 2026** is speculation. For the systems side of the same material, start with [progression and builds](/guides/progression-and-builds/); for the release and edition details, see the [release date page](/release-date/).
