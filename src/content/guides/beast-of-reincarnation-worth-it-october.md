On **October 20, 2026**, Steam App **2001760** still listed Beast of Reincarnation at **$59.99** with a **0%** discount. The game has been out since **August 4, 2026**. Reviews that morning were Mixed: **3,780** positive out of **6,399**. That is the number to argue with, not a memory of launch week.

The question is not whether the game is a 10. Players with long files disagree with each other. The question is whether **$59.99** buys a finished action RPG you can set up and finish, or a game you are still supposed to wait on. The v1.1.01 notes answer the second half. The major post-launch cycle was described as closing, with one more patch planned around October 19. If you are waiting for a different game, you are waiting on a note that already said the frequent updates are ending.

## The price, plainly

**$59.99** is the standard edition on the US Steam listing checked October 20. There was no discount. The Deluxe upgrade is a separate **$9.99** DLC, App **4146830**, which is how the **$69.99** Deluxe total is built: black and brown Shiba skins for Koo, the Oni's Hat for Emma, Emma's sword Big Dipper, **100,000** Amber, and vegetable seedlings. None of that changes combat if the base game is the thing you are unsure about. Buy the upgrade after the first zone feels good, not before.

Day-one Xbox Game Pass Ultimate and PC Game Pass were part of the launch pitch. If you already pay for one of those, the **$59.99** question is the wrong question. Install it there, set the field of view, and fight the first real enemy. Steam is the full-price Windows listing. Game Pass is a different door. Do not pay full price on Steam to answer a question Game Pass would have answered in an evening, if you already have the subscription.

Platforms on the store side are Windows for the Steam app. The game also shipped on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. A Mixed Steam score is a Steam score. It is not an automatic console review.

## What the review count is actually saying

**3,780** positive out of **6,399** is about **59%** positive. Steam calls that Mixed. It is not Mostly Negative, and it is not the Very Positive scores sitting on other games that launched in the same season. It is a split, and the long reviews on October 20 still show the same split.

A positive review at **1,733** minutes called the gameplay good and somewhat unique, and said the negative pile had been unfair. A positive review at **1,310** minutes compared it to Sekiro and NieR and singled out Koo. A positive review at **1,567** minutes said the launch camera on Game Pass was too close, and that a patch fixed it. A negative review at **1,315** minutes said the bones were there and the story kept undermining them, and marked itself as full of spoilers.

Those four files are all past the refund window. They are not "two hours and mad." They are people who played and landed on opposite sides. If your bar is "no one who finished it dislikes the story," this game misses that bar. If your bar is "the combat and the dog are enough, and the camera can be pulled back," the same reviews support a buy.

## What you can fix in the menu now

v1.1.01, the eighth patch since launch, added a Field of View slider and a toggle for cutscene skip prompts. It also fixed subtitles sticking after a skip, a soft lock from rapid skipping, Entanglement Overdrive canceling into a lock-on toggle, and the camera locking onto Koo if you canceled a strongbox unlock during a Bloom Art. If an August review is your only source, you are reviewing an older camera.

The same patch added **Hasten to the Capital** after the Great Wall Terraces Zone. It can bypass Pilgrim's Zone, Matsukawa Colony Zone, and Fuji Frontier Zone. On a New Game run, achievements from those zones cannot be earned if you take the skip. They can still be earned on New Game+ by taking the Mountain Route. That is a length control with a cost, which matters because "the game is too long and samey" is part of why the score is Mixed. You can shorten a story run. You should not shorten the only file you care about collecting.

Minimum PC requirements on the store page: Windows 10 or 11, i5-8400 or Ryzen 5 3600, **16 GB** RAM, GTX 1070 8 GB or RX 580 8 GB, **45 GB** on an SSD, low 1080p around 40 frames with DLSS or FSR. Recommended: Windows 11, i7-12700K or Ryzen 7 5800X, RTX 3060 12 GB or RX 6700 XT 12 GB, medium 1080p around 60 with upscaling. If your machine is under the minimum, **$59.99** is a bad experiment. The patch improved effect load. It did not change the SSD requirement.

## Who should buy it this week

Buy it if you want the one-person, one-dog combat loop and you can live with a story some long-time players reject. The systems that reviews praise — parry, Koo, builds, movement — are in the shipped game. The camera complaint has a slider. The length complaint has a skip, if you accept the achievement trade.

Buy it on Game Pass first if that subscription is already active. A Mixed score is exactly the situation a subscription is for. Full price is for the player who already knows they want Emma and Koo on a Steam library, cloud saves included.

Wait if you need a sale. Nothing on the October 20 listing said a discount was coming. Waiting is still rational at **$59.99** when about two in five reviews are not positive. Just do not tell yourself you are waiting for a content overhaul. The studio's own note said the major update phase was closing.

Skip it if the story is the main reason you play, and a 20-hour negative review from someone who wanted to like it is enough of a warning. That review exists. Pretending the Mixed score is only "people who didn't get it" ignores a **1,315**-minute negative file.

## A number to keep next to the price

**46** achievements. **94.6%** of players have the early purifier pop. **27.2%** have the Beast of Reincarnation achievement. **7.3%** have Best Buddy. The game is finished enough that a quarter of the achievement population reached the title fight's pop, and unfinished enough in players' hands that most never did. That is a normal single-player curve, not evidence of a broken install.

Languages on the store page include full English and Japanese audio, plus French, German, Spanish, Korean, Portuguese (Brazil), Simplified and Traditional Chinese, and Italian. Controller support is listed as full, with Steam Cloud and Family Sharing. None of those boxes make the Mixed score go away. They do mean a household that already shares a library can try the game without a second **$59.99** purchase, which is the cleanest way to answer "is the dog enough" without arguing with strangers.

The [support-cycle note](/guides/beast-of-reincarnation-support-is-ending/) is the page to read if you are waiting on patches. The [field-of-view page](/guides/beast-of-reincarnation-fov-and-skip-prompts/) is the page to read if the camera is the only thing between you and a purchase. Neither page changes the **$59.99**. They change what you are buying it for.

If you buy, play the first zone on the difficulty you actually want. Story, Normal, and Hard have been there since launch. Very Hard and Ruthless arrived in v1.1.0. Do not start on the newest hardest mode to prove the Mixed reviews wrong. Start where you can see the parry, keep Koo's art for a weakness you can read, and decide after the Great Wall Terraces whether this file is a Mountain Route or a hasten. That decision is worth more than another hour of store-page scrolling.
