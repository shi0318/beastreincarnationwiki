Beyond the Nushi, Beast of Reincarnation scatters **elite enemies** – tougher Golems and Malefacts that are optional, drop better loot and often guard a blade or a key item. None are missable; you can return in free roam. Here are the elites in the first three regions with their levels.

## How to fight elites

- **Heavy Golems** telegraph slowly – they are parry practice and Flow batteries.
- **Agile Golems** punish greed; parry the first hit and let Koo interrupt the rest.
- **Lupine Malefacts** come with packs – thin the pack with arrows or Koo before you engage the leader.
- Clear elites that sit on the story path when you pass them, and group the rest into one cleanup trip per region.
