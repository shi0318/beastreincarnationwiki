One boss complaint keeps appearing in Beast of Reincarnation reviews: some Nushi are huge, and their headbutts and slams come from **above the camera**. The parry that works on a side swing fails here because you cannot see the strike begin.

## The answer

- **Step toward the boss's hip, not backward.** A backward roll keeps you inside the hitbox.
- Treat overhead slams as **dodge checks**, not parries. Once the head is down, you have a punish.
- If the combo continues with a normal swing, **that** second hit is the parry.
- Use the **camera distance** setting (Close/Medium/Far, added in v1.0.8) – Far makes big Nushi much easier to read.

## Overheads by boss

| Boss | Overhead / unparryable | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Rangifer | Rematch jump attack | Do not stand under it |
| Taurus | Jump-away shockwave slam | Step out of the wave line |
| Ursa | Leaping lunge into rock pillars | Dodge sideways as it leaves the ground |
| Erymanth | Tusk Slam / Floral Slam | Reposition during the wind-up |
| Shidou | Vertical tree slam | Perfect-dodge the landing, then counter |
| Corvus | Divebomb | Dodge or parry, then restrain with Rangifer |

## Difficulty notes

On **Ruthless** (v1.1.0) any hit kills Emma, so overheads become the main cause of death. Learn them on Hard first. v1.1.0 also overhauled attack patterns for some Nushi variants – older videos may show different timings.
