Recent reviews keep landing on the same boss complaint. Some Nushi are simple. Some are huge, and a headbutt comes from above the camera. The parry that feels great on a side swing does nothing useful here, because you cannot see the strike start.

## The answer

Step toward the boss's hip, not backward. A backward roll is how the hitbox still finds you. The headbutt is a vertical commit. Once the head is down, you have a punish. Before it is down, you do not have a parry.

If the same boss also has a normal swing later in the combo, that second hit is the parry. People die because they spend the parry on the first motion.

This is a feel note from people who have fought the roster, not a frame chart. 1.1.0's Nightmare mode makes the same tell lethal. Learn it on Hard first. The [Nushi list](/bosses/) is still the roster page. This page is only the overhead.
