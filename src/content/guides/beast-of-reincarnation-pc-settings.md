The official PC target for **Beast of Reincarnation** is a 64-bit Windows 10/11 system with **16 GB RAM**, DirectX 12 and **45 GB of SSD space**. The minimum and recommended GPU targets are different, so use the preset that matches the hardware instead of copying a console setting.

## Minimum and recommended targets

| Target | CPU | GPU | Memory | Stated target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Minimum | Intel i5-8400 or Ryzen 5 3600 | GTX 1070 8 GB or RX 580 8 GB | 16 GB | 1080p Low around 40 FPS with upscaling |
| Recommended | Intel i7-12700K or Ryzen 7 5800X | RTX 3060 12 GB or RX 6700 XT 12 GB | 16 GB | 1080p Medium around 60 FPS with upscaling |

## A sensible first settings pass

Install on an SSD, start with the published preset, then change only one option at a time. Upscaling should be tested in a busy combat scene rather than an empty campsite. If frame generation is available in the current patch, compare input feel as well as the displayed frame rate.

The [PC performance fixes guide](/guides/pc-performance-settings-fixes/) tracks patch changes, while [PC system requirements](/guides/pc-system-requirements/) is the compact hardware reference.

## Steam Deck boundary

The current record does not treat Beast of Reincarnation as Steam Deck Verified. A handheld can still be a personal compatibility test, but that result should not be presented as a universal supported configuration. Record OS version, wattage, resolution and patch when reporting a result.
