Game Freak's PC notes for **v1.1.01** do not bury the schedule. After the eighth post-launch patch, the studio wrote that the current update cycle is coming to a close. The next patch, planned for **October 19** with the final date still to be confirmed in that post, would be the last patch in this phase of major post-launch updates. Frequent updates would end. Support tickets through the Fictions site would not.

That is a planning fact, not a rumor about the game being abandoned overnight. It changes how you should spend the next session. A build you are waiting to "get fixed later" is a build you should judge on v1.1.01, plus whatever that closing patch actually shipped. Beast of Reincarnation has been on sale since **August 4, 2026**. On **October 20, 2026** the Steam price was still **$59.99**, discount **0%**, and the review score was Mixed: **3,780** positive reviews out of **6,399**.

## What "final patch in this phase" means

It means the studio described an end to frequent, major post-launch updates. It does not mean servers switch off, saves die, or the store page comes down. Steam still lists single-player, achievements, full controller support, adjustable difficulty, and Steam Cloud. The game is a local action RPG. A closed patch cycle is not an online shutdown.

It also does not mean every bug you have ever seen is now permanent. The same note invites players to keep filing feedback and support tickets. What it does mean is that you should not build a play plan around a ninth, tenth, and eleventh balance pass. The notes say that kind of cadence is ending.

v1.1.01 itself is already a large quality pass, not a content drop. It added Hasten to the Capital after the Great Wall Terraces, a field-of-view slider, a skip-prompt toggle, cutscene timing fixes, a soft-lock fix for rapid skipping, an Entanglement Overdrive cancel fix, a Koo camera lock fix during strongboxes, a fix for Koo using items on staggered enemies, performance work on effect generation, and collision and submersion fixes across zones. If your complaint is on that list, install the patch before you write the game off. If your complaint is "I want a new zone," that is not what this closing note promised.

## What to finish while the build is stable

Steam tracks **46** achievements. On October 20 the common ones were already nearly universal and the late ones were not. **ACH_PURIFIER** was at **94.6%**. **ACH_ENCAMPMENT** was **92.5%**. **ACH_GROWTH_OF_IMPURITY** was **83.0%**. At the other end, **ACH_BEST_BUDDY** was **7.3%**, **ACH_THE_ULTIMATE_VESSEL** **7.4%**, and **ACH_FAMILY_DINING_TABLE** **7.7%**. **ACH_KENGO** was **10.9%**. **ACH_BEAST_OF_REINCARNATION** and **ACH_LIVE** were both **27.2%**.

Those rates are the real shape of the player base two and a half months after launch. Most people who own the game have done the early clear. About one in four has the achievement tied to the Beast of Reincarnation name. Fewer than one in ten has the rarest pops in that sample. A closing patch cycle does not move those numbers for you. Playing the file does.

If you are still in the first hours, the useful work is unchanged: learn the parry, take the full dodge roll, keep one Bloom Art that matches the weakness you can see, and do not skip Pilgrim's Zone, Matsukawa Colony Zone, or Fuji Frontier Zone on a New Game file if you want those achievements. [Hasten to the Capital](/guides/beast-of-reincarnation-hasten-to-the-capital/) is a story tool. It is a bad tool if the achievement list is the reason you are still installed.

## How to read the Mixed score now

A Mixed score at **3,780 / 6,399** is not a launch-day pile-on anymore. The game has been out since August 4. Reviews checked on October 20 still include players with more than a thousand minutes who recommend it, and players with a similar amount of time who do not. One positive review at **1,310** minutes compared the combat to Sekiro and NieR. One negative review at **1,315** minutes said the bones were there and the story kept undermining them. Both of those players finished enough of the game to have an opinion. A patch cycle ending does not settle that argument.

What it does settle is the "wait for the game to become a different game" plan. v1.1.0 already added harder difficulty modes. v1.1.01 added the story skip, the camera slider, and the skip-prompt toggle. The notes for v1.1.01 say the next patch closes the major phase. If you need the camera fixed, it has a slider. If you need the story shorter, the Capital shortcut exists, with an achievement cost. If you need the story rewritten, that is not on the list.

## A closing-cycle checklist

Use this before you decide the **$59.99** listing is or is not for you. The price on October 20 had no discount. Waiting for a sale is a store decision. Waiting for a new combat system is not supported by the note the studio posted.

1. Confirm the PC build is v1.1.01 or newer. The eighth patch is the one with the field of view and the Capital shortcut. Playing a launch build in late October and blaming the current game is a version mistake.
2. Set field of view and the skip prompt before you judge combat. Those are menu fixes. [The settings pass](/guides/beast-of-reincarnation-fov-and-skip-prompts/) is one fight long.
3. Decide whether this file is a story run or an achievement run. Do not use Hasten to the Capital on the only New Game file if zone achievements still matter.
4. File a ticket for a bug that is not on the v1.1.01 fix list. The notes still point players at Fictions support. They do not promise a weekly balance patch after the closing update.
5. Judge the **$59.99** price against the game you can play this week, not against a roadmap the notes said is ending.

The store page still describes a fusion of real-time and turn-based combat, Emma and Koo, and a post-apocalyptic Japan. Minimum spec is 16 GB of RAM, a GTX 1070 or RX 580, and 45 GB on an SSD, targeting low 1080p around 40 frames with upscaling. Recommended is an i7-12700K or Ryzen 7 5800X and an RTX 3060 or RX 6700 XT for medium 1080p around 60. None of that hardware advice changes because the patch cadence is slowing down.

If you are already in the Capital and the only thing left is a rare achievement at a 7% unlock rate, keep the file. The closing note is about updates, not about deleting your save. If you have not started, and you were waiting for the Mixed score to flip because of a content season, read the v1.1.01 post again. The season they described is the one that is ending.

Deluxe buyers should make the same read. The **$9.99** upgrade adds Shiba skins, the Oni's Hat, Big Dipper, **100,000** Amber, and seedlings. It does not add a new patch cadence. Spend it if you already like the dog and the sword. Do not spend it as a vote for future zones the notes did not promise.
