Story mode is a damage setting. The field-of-view slider is a camera setting. Players keep swapping them because a close camera makes a fair hit look cheap, and a cheap hit makes a close camera feel like the only problem. v1.1.01 separated the tools. Use the one that matches the complaint.

Beast of Reincarnation launched **August 4, 2026** with Story, Normal, and Hard. v1.1.0 later added harder modes. v1.1.01, the eighth patch, put Field of View in Options and a skip-prompt toggle in Settings. On **October 20, 2026** the Steam price was still **$59.99**, and reviews were Mixed: **3,780** of **6,399**. A review at **1,567** minutes said the Game Pass camera was too close at launch and a later patch fixed the feel. That review is about the frame, not about Story mode.

## What each lever does

Story, Normal, and Hard are the launch difficulties. They are there so a hit you already saw can hurt less or more. They do not move the camera back. A player who dies because the swing started outside the frame will die on Story too, just a little later, and will learn the wrong timing.

Field of View is the October patch's answer to that frame. Pull it until the windup fits and Emma is still readable. Stop before the parry flash becomes a speck. Then fight one normal enemy. If the swing is visible and you still miss the parry, that is a timing problem. Story mode is allowed after that test. It is not allowed instead of it.

The skip-prompt toggle is a third lever. Hide the prompt if you watch events. Leave it if you skip them. It does not change damage and it does not change the fight camera. A subtitle that used to stick after a skip is on the fixed list in the same patch. Do not drop to Story because a cinematic annoyed you.

## A one-fight test

1. Confirm v1.1.01 or newer. The slider is not on the launch build.
2. Stay on Normal. Move Field of View. Parry one beast you can already reach.
3. If you saw the swing and parried it, leave the difficulty alone.
4. If you saw the swing and the punish still killed you, then consider Story. You have isolated damage from framing.
5. If you still cannot see the swing, the slider is not far enough, or the install is not on the patch. Do not touch Story yet.

Very Hard and Ruthless arrived in v1.1.0. They are for a file that already wins on Hard. Starting there to prove the Mixed reviews wrong is how a camera complaint becomes a refund. The [worth-it page](/guides/beast-of-reincarnation-worth-it-october/) is the purchase argument. This page is the first menu.

## What else feels like difficulty and is not

Canceling Entanglement Overdrive used to toggle lock-on. Canceling a strongbox unlock during Koo's Bloom Art used to lock the camera on the dog. Both are marked fixed in v1.1.01. If the camera snaps to Koo at a chest, that is the strongbox bug, not Story mode. Update, then retest the chest before you rewrite the difficulty.

Red attacks are still dodges. A wider frame makes the red flash easier to see. It does not turn a red attack into a parry. Story mode does not either. The [early build](/guides/beast-of-reincarnation-best-early-build/) still starts with the full dodge roll and one Bloom Art you can land. Set the camera so that art is visible. Then decide if Normal hurts too much.

**46** achievements. **ACH_PURIFIER** at **94.6%** is the early pop. **ACH_BEAST_OF_REINCARNATION** at **27.2%** is the late one. Neither requires Story mode. A file that only works on Story can still be a finished file. A file that never widened the camera cannot tell you whether Normal was the problem.

Minimum spec wants an SSD, **16 GB** of RAM, and a GTX 1070 or RX 580, aiming at about 40 frames at 1080p with upscaling. A hitch that hides the swing is a performance problem. Field of view will not fix a machine under that line. Lower the preset before you lower the difficulty, and lower the difficulty before you decide the parry is random.

The Mixed score includes people who finished the game and disliked the story, and people who liked the dog and the combat. Story mode does not settle that. A visible swing does. Set the frame, fight one enemy, and only then touch the damage.


## One more pass before you close the menu

The numbers on this page were checked against the October 20, 2026 Steam listing, not against a launch-week memory. Price, review count, achievement total, and the patch lines named above are the facts to plan a session around. A thumbnail that contradicts them is a thumbnail. Play the build you have. Write down the result in the game's own words. Then decide whether the next door, the next bind, or the next purchase is the one this page was about.

If the machine is under the minimum spec printed on the store page, fix that before you fix the tactic. An SSD requirement is a disk requirement. A RAM number is a RAM number. A driver popup is a driver popup. None of those become optional because a guide got longer. The tactic still matters after the machine holds a frame. It does not matter during a hitch you mistook for an enemy tell.

Stop when the one test in the list above has a yes or a no. A second system, a second cosmetic, and a second difficulty change in the same minute will make the yes useless. Come back for the second change after the first one is written down. The rest of the site can wait until that note exists.
