The last 30 days on the US Steam page, checked **October 25, 2026**, were **68%** positive out of **109** English reviews. The page still called the game Mixed. The price was still **$59.99**, with no discount on that page.

Sixty-eight percent sounds like a collapse if you put it next to a larger, older total and forget the sample size. One hundred and nine reviews is a month of people who wrote something. It is not every owner. It is not every language. It is not a patch note. A handful of angry files can move a percentage that small. A handful of defenders can move it back. Use it as a mood, then go look at the patch you can actually install.

## What a 30-day line is allowed to mean

It means English reviewers in that window were not enthusiastic as a group. **68%** positive is not Mostly Positive. It is the recent slice of a Mixed page. The all-time English line on the same page was **1,585** reviews, also Mixed. The recent line did not invent a new genre of complaint. It kept the old one warm: camera, length, story, and whether the dog is enough.

It does not mean v1.1.01 failed. That patch added a field-of-view slider, a skip-prompt toggle, cutscene fixes, and Hasten to the Capital after the Great Wall Terraces. Players reviewing in the last 30 days may be on that patch, or they may be describing a file they started in August and never updated. You cannot see their version in the percentage. You can see yours. Check it.

It does not mean you should refund a finished file. One hundred and nine people are not your save. If you are in the Capital and the only pain is a rare achievement, the month did not delete your progress. If you have not started, the month is a reason to try before you pay full price, not a reason to declare the combat dead.

## What to test instead of refreshing the percentage

Set Field of View and fight one beast on Normal. If you can see the windup, the recent reviews did not hide a camera you cannot fix. If you cannot see it, you are not on the patch, or the slider is not far enough. Story mode is the wrong next click. It changes damage. It does not move the camera.

Do not hasten past Pilgrim's Zone, Matsukawa Colony Zone, and Fuji Frontier on a New Game file if you care about those achievements. The skip is a story tool. A Mixed month does not make it free. The Explosive Arrow manual still lives with the Keeper Golems in Fuji Frontier. New Game Plus can pick it up on the Mountain Route after v1.1.01. A skipped New Game file cannot.

Family Sharing is the clean experiment. One borrowed hour answers the 109-review argument for your household. Cloud saves follow the account that owns the progress. Do not start a long boss on a borrow. Do not buy the Deluxe upgrade to settle a percentage. The base game is the question. The upgrade is a hat and a sword skin after the question is yes.
