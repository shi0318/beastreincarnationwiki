Short answer: **a typical Beast of Reincarnation playthrough runs about 16 to 24 hours**, and roughly **one owner in four gets as deep as the achievement list tracks.** Those two numbers come from different places and they are both measurable, so this page keeps them apart.

Game Freak has never published a campaign length for Beast of Reincarnation. What *is* public is how long the people writing Steam reviews had played when they wrote them, and what percentage of all owners holds each achievement — including the six named after the Nushi. Put together, those give a far more honest answer than a single "X hours" figure.

## The playtime numbers

Measured across a 400-review sample of Steam reviews, using the playtime each reviewer had logged at the moment they posted:

| Measure | Hours |
| --- | --- |
| **Median playtime at review** | **16.4** |
| Average playtime at review | 20.0 |
| Lower quartile (25% had played less) | 3.7 |
| Upper quartile (25% had played more) | 29.9 |
| 90th percentile | 43.2 |
| **Median total playtime now** | **24.0** |

The gap between the two bolded rows is the useful part. Reviewers post at a median of **16.4 hours** but their accounts now sit at a median of **24.0 hours** — so the average reviewer wrote their verdict with roughly two-thirds of their eventual run behind them, then kept going. Treat 16 hours as "far enough to have an opinion" and the low-to-mid 20s as a realistic finish figure for a normal run.

## Where players actually spend their hours

The same sample, grouped:

| Playtime at review | Share of reviewers |
| --- | --- |
| Under 2 hours | 16.8% |
| 2–5 hours | 12.2% |
| 5–10 hours | 11.2% |
| 10–20 hours | 15.8% |
| **20–40 hours** | **32.2%** |
| Over 40 hours | 11.8% |

Two clusters, and they tell opposite stories. **The largest single group — just under a third — sits at 20 to 40 hours**, which is a full campaign plus side content. But **16.8% posted under two hours**, the Steam refund window, and that is an unusually high figure. It lines up with the store's **Mixed** rating: of **5,925 reviews**, 3,467 are positive and 2,458 are negative, or **58.5% positive**. A meaningful slice of the negative side is people who decided inside two hours, largely on performance and first-hour friction rather than on length. The [player reception guide](/guides/should-you-buy-player-reception/) and [PC performance fixes](/guides/pc-performance-settings-fixes/) cover that separately.

Read the table the other way and it is encouraging: **44% of reviewers had passed 20 hours.** Nobody is running out of game.

## The completion funnel: where players stop

Steam publishes a global unlock percentage for every achievement, across all owners rather than only reviewers. One caveat first, and it matters: **Valve hides the description on every Nushi-named achievement.** What each one actually requires is not published, so this table lists the achievement names as Steam gives them and marks the hidden ones. The percentage is still a hard number — it tells you how many owners have reached that point, whatever triggers it.

| Achievement | Description on Steam | Owners who have it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First Fire | Discover your first campsite | 92.6% |
| Blight-Touched | Enhance an attribute for the first time | 83.0% |
| **Rangifer's End** | Hidden | **53.5%** |
| Place of Return | Discover your first shrine | 51.5% |
| **Lacerta's End** | Hidden | **43.9%** |
| **Taurus's End** | Hidden | **34.9%** |
| **Corvus's End** | Hidden | **28.4%** |
| Green Thumb | Obtain every type of seedling | 26.8% |
| Koo de Grâce | Hidden | 25.7% |
| **Ursa's End** | Hidden | **25.1%** |
| Shidou & Nue | Hidden | 25.0% |
| **Erymanth's End** | Hidden | **24.8%** |
| Kunai & Kirin | Hidden | 24.6% |
| Life After the End | Hidden | 24.4% |
| The End of Reincarnation | Hidden | 24.4% |
| Duty Fulfilled | Unlock every achievement | 2.8% |

Three things fall out of that table.

**Rangifer is the real filter.** 92.6% of owners light a campfire; 53.5% hold Rangifer's End. Nearly **four in ten people who start the game never get that far.** This site's hands-on coverage puts Rangifer as the first Nushi you meet, and it is fought twice — so if you are stuck there, you are not an outlier. See the [Rangifer fight guide](/guides/nushi-boss-fight/) and the [parry, guard and dodge guide](/guides/parry-guard-dodge-controls-guide/).

**Rangifer's End is more common than finding your first shrine** (53.5% vs 51.5%), which puts that encounter very early — before the point where most people have settled into the exploration loop.

**After Corvus the curve flattens into a cluster.** Corvus 28.4% → Ursa 25.1% → Erymanth 24.8%, and then seven separate achievements sit inside a single point of each other between 25.7% and 24.4%. Once a player is past Corvus they very nearly always reach the bottom of that cluster. The drop-off is concentrated in the first half; the back half is a straight run. So "how long to beat" has a hidden second answer: **the hard part is the first six hours, not the last six.**

## Adding hours after the credits

Full completion is a different scale of commitment, and the rates make that obvious:

- Obtain every type of seedling — **26.8%**
- Obtain the manuals for every arrow and bolt type — **12.6%**
- Obtain every blade — **6.0%**
- Discover every campsite — **4.8%**
- Unlock every achievement — **2.8%**

The collectible sets are what separates a 24-hour run from the 40-plus hours that 11.8% of reviewers have logged. The longest single account in the sample sits at **377.9 hours**, which is a farming figure rather than a story one — see [level 100 farming](/guides/level-100-farming-guide/) and the [amber technique point farm](/guides/amber-technique-points-farm/) for what that time goes into. [New Game Plus](/guides/new-game-plus-guide/) is the other multiplier.

## So how long will *your* run take?

Based on the distributions above, not on a guess:

- **Main progression, playing normally: 16–24 hours.** That is the median-at-review to median-total band, and it is where the bulk of finishers sit.
- **Rushing it, skipping side content: 12–15 hours.** Plausible from the lower quartile, but you will be under-levelled for the later Nushi.
- **Main progression plus most side content: 25–35 hours.** The 32.2% cluster.
- **Everything, including collectible sets: 45 hours and up.** Only about one player in nine gets here, and only 2.8% finish the achievement list.

Difficulty matters to all of these, and Beast of Reincarnation lets you change it — the [difficulty modes guide](/guides/difficulty-modes-guide/) covers what each setting does. Time spent stuck is the largest variable in every figure on this page.

## What these numbers are not

Worth stating plainly, because a lot of "how long to beat" pages hide it:

- **Reviewers are not all players.** People who post reviews skew toward people who played more. The real median across every owner is lower than 16.4 hours.
- **Playtime is wall-clock, not progress.** It includes menus, deaths, farming and idling.
- **Achievement percentages count owners, not finishers.** They include people who launched the game once, which is exactly why the first-campsite figure is 92.6% rather than 100%.
- **Hidden descriptions stay hidden here.** Valve does not publish what any of the Nushi-named achievements require, so this page does not say what unlocks them or which one marks the credits. It reports the names and the rates.
- **No official length exists.** Game Freak has not published one, and neither this page nor any other has access to one.
- Percentages here were read on **September 2, 2026**, four weeks after the August 4 launch. Completion rates only ever rise.
