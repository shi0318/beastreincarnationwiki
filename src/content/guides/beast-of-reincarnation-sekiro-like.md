Reviewers who like **Beast of Reincarnation** reach for **Sekiro**, **Black Myth: Wukong**, **Stellar Blade**, and **Nier: Automata**. That is player shorthand for parry-heavy action with a stylish presentation, not a studio claim.

## What is actually in this game

Emma parries and uses Blade Arts. Koo adds Bloom Arts. Nushi are regional bosses with Steam-named endings. The world is blighted Japan with campsites, not a FromSoftware castle.

If you want Sekiro's posture war, this is adjacent, not a clone. If you want Nier's story density, Mixed reviews already warn the plot feels slow.
