Beast of Reincarnation is out — it unlocked at **00:00 UTC on 4 August 2026** — so this page has been rewritten from post-launch coverage of the actual fight and no longer speculates about it. The **Nushi** are the game's major bosses: powerful Malefect encounters, each ruling a region. The first one you meet is **Rangifer**, an enormous stag/plant hybrid.

The detail that shapes everything below: **you fight Rangifer twice**. The first time is part of the **Prowling Nushi** quest, in a crater. The second is in its sealed lair, where it is stronger and gains a second stage. The same tactics carry over, so treat fight one as a rehearsal for fight two.

If you have not read the systems primer yet, the [beginner's guide](/guides/launch-day-beginner-guide/) covers the parry-to-FP loop this fight leans on heavily.

Levels are the figures GamesRadar's Rangifer guide reports; if the first attempt feels impossible, the fix is usually two or three levels rather than better execution. Kill nearby enemies for XP and come back.

## Before you swing: take the arena's camp

The centre of Rangifer's first arena has a **tall building with a campsite on top**, plus **armor** up there. Unlock the camp before you commit to the fight. This is the general rule the launch coverage keeps repeating — most boss arenas have a campsite nearby for resting, saving and applying upgrades, and some are not obvious, so you do not want to discover it after a death with no checkpoint set.

## Open with a free assassination

From that central camp, use Emma's **hair bridge** (the Extend ability) to get **above** Rangifer, then jump down for an **air assassination**. GamesRadar reports this removes a huge chunk of its health immediately — which is a much better use of your opening seconds than walking in swinging.

Then, before it closes distance:

- **Empty your ranged ammo.** Rangifer is almost entirely melee-focused, so the **bow** and **crossbow** are close to free damage if you keep space. Use both.
- **Stay elevated where you can.** Most of Rangifer's moveset hits *downward*, so height meaningfully degrades its ability to reach you. Practise making your own grapple points here even though phase one does not demand it — the second fight does.

## Fire is the answer

Rangifer is **weak to fire** in both encounters. In practice that means:

- **Koo's Higanbana: Seeds** — a spread of fiery projectiles that builds the **burn** status — is the single most valuable bloom art to bring, and GamesRadar rates it among the best skills in the game generally.
- For the rematch you can also pick up **Fire Arrows** from **Brad** back at the Cleanse Walker, giving Emma a fire option of her own.
- Caveat for fight two: Rangifer keeps the fire weakness but becomes **more resistant to the Burn status effect**, so lean on raw fire damage rather than assuming burn ticks will carry you.

## Parry the antlers

Rangifer's most frequent attack is a **swing of its antlers**, and despite the creature's size, **this attack is parryable**. Parry it deliberately, for two reasons: the parry builds **Koo's FP** so you can keep casting fire, and the swing **leaves Rangifer's head lowered**, which is your safest melee window in the whole fight.

That is the loop: parry the antlers, punish the lowered head, spend FP on fire, repeat.

## The attacks that will kill you

**Red glow — the four-sweep combo.** If Rangifer's body takes on a **red glow**, it is about to perform **four antler sweeps followed by a stomp**. These are fast, hard to parry, and can take Emma from full health to dead. Do not try to trade. Your options, per GamesRadar: have **Koo use a binding attack to interrupt it**, escape using **grapple points you made earlier**, or evade out repeatedly.

**The rear-and-stomp.** If Rangifer rears up and stamps, it produces a small AOE impact with **sharp roots rising** from the ground. Clear space rather than standing at its feet.

**Tree summon.** If it backs away, yells, and slams its head into the ground, it is spawning **trees erupting from the forest floor**, telegraphed by **red circles** on the ground. Move off the markers.

## Phase two turns its own attack into your mobility

In the lair fight, reducing Rangifer to zero health does not end it — it unlocks new abilities and a **fresh health bar**. The useful wrinkle: in phase two the summoned trees include **non-damaging trees that last longer**, and Emma can use them as **grapple points**. Several of Rangifer's phase-two attacks are very hard to avoid on flat ground, so track where those trees appear and treat them as escape routes rather than scenery.

Between the two fights, **upgrade your weapons**. The rematch lasts considerably longer, and the gap gives you time to farm the **Amber** you need for it.

## What you get for it

GameSpot's beginner guide reports the first boss rewards the **Sakura Storm** skill, which releases a row of Sakura after four consecutive slashes and can be used to leap out of the way.
