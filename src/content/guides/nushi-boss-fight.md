**Closer to confirmed**

- The game is built around Emma's sword combat plus Koo command support.
- Official copy emphasizes Bosses that rule the blighted world and power capture as a progression fantasy.
- Hybrid combat mixes real-time action with turn-based RPG elements, so boss fights may include both reaction windows and command timing.

**Still speculation**

- Exact phase splits, HP thresholds, arena floods, attack names, and reward routes.

## How we expect the fight to feel

Based on official combat pillars - not playtests - a major boss is likely to punish greed, reward patience, and force coordination with Koo rather than pure button-mashing. A practical launch approach:

1. Enter with healing and Koo resources available, not half empty.
2. Spend the first attempt learning telegraphs instead of maximizing damage.
3. Test whether Koo is better used for interrupt, peel, or burst on recovery frames.
4. Note whether the arena changes mid-fight; official marketing leans hard on shifting blighted environments.
