The Nushi is the first true wall most players hit in Beast of Reincarnation. It punishes greed, rewards patience, and its second phase changes the arena in a way that catches unprepared players off guard. This guide breaks the fight into its three phases, lists every attack you need to read, and pins down the exact windows where Koo can pile on damage safely.

## Before You Engage

Come into the arena with your healing charges topped off and Koo's bond meter at least half full. The Nushi fight is long — closer to five minutes than two — so raw damage matters less than survivability. Bring a weapon with reach if you have one; several Nushi attacks force you to disengage, and a short blade wastes your return windows.

Two things to check first:

- **Stamina management.** You cannot facetank Nushi. Keep at least one dodge's worth of stamina in reserve at all times.
- **Koo positioning.** Send Koo to flank early. The Nushi tracks Emma almost exclusively in phase one, which leaves its side open.

## Phase One: Reading the Sweep

The opening phase is a rhythm check. Nushi has four attacks here, and all of them are readable:

- **Horizontal sweep** — telegraphed by a slow shoulder dip to the left. Dodge *into* it, toward the tail, not away. Dodging back keeps you in range for the follow-up.
- **Overhead slam** — both arms raise. This is your best punish. Sidestep and you get a full three-hit combo on the recovery.
- **Lunge bite** — Nushi pulls its head back. Parry this one; the timing is generous and a clean parry staggers it briefly.
- **Roar** — harmless damage-wise but it buffs its next attack's speed. Use the roar window to heal or reposition, never to attack.

Whittle it to roughly 65% and it transitions.

## Phase Two: The Flooded Arena

At the phase break Nushi floods the arena floor, slowing your movement and dodges. This is the phase that ends most runs. Movement is sluggish, so you must rely on parries far more than dodges here.

The key new attack is the **tail sweep into water geyser** — Nushi slaps the water, then geysers erupt where the splashes landed about a second later. Watch the water surface, not the boss. The geyser tells are visible on the floor before they fire.

Koo becomes essential now. When Koo lands a flanking bite the Nushi flinches, interrupting its geyser chains. Time your Koo command right after the tail slap to break the sequence entirely.

## Phase Three: Enraged

Below 30% Nushi enrages: attacks chain longer and the recovery windows shrink. Stop looking for big punishes. Take one hit per opening and reset. Greed here is what kills players who otherwise had the fight won.

The enrage does not add new attacks — it only speeds up and lengthens the phase-one moveset. If you learned the tells earlier, this phase is about discipline, not new knowledge. Keep Koo flanking, chip safely, and the Nushi falls.

## Rewards & Follow-Up

Beating Nushi opens the river route east and drops the material you need for your first weapon upgrade tier. Head to the [river exploration route](/guides/emma-koo-river-exploration/) next — it's the natural progression and the enemies there are tuned for your post-Nushi power level.
