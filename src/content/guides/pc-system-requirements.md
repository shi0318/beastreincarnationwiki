Before pre-load, Fictions and Game Freak said an earlier published PC-specification list contained mistakes and that the updated Steam store requirements were the correct ones. The tables below reflect that current first-party Steam listing, re-checked on **2026-08-08**. Both tiers name a target resolution, a target frame rate, and the upscaler assumed to hit it, which changes what "recommended" actually promises.

## Minimum requirements

| Component | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| **OS** | Windows 10 / 11 (64-bit) |
| **CPU** | Intel Core i5-8400 or AMD Ryzen 5 3600 |
| **RAM** | 16 GB |
| **GPU** | NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1070 (8 GB) or AMD Radeon RX 580 (8 GB) |
| **DirectX** | Version 12 |
| **Storage** | 45 GB available space |

Official note, verbatim: **"Low 1080p @ 40fps w/ DLSS (RTX 20-50 Series) or FSR1 (Performance). SSD required."**

## Recommended requirements

| Component | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| **OS** | Windows 11 (64-bit) |
| **CPU** | Intel Core i7-12700K or AMD Ryzen 7 5800X |
| **RAM** | 16 GB |
| **GPU** | NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060 (12 GB) or AMD Radeon RX 6700 XT (12 GB) |
| **DirectX** | Version 12 |
| **Storage** | 45 GB available space |

Official note, verbatim: **"Med 1080p @ 60fps w/ DLSS or FSR3 (Performance). SSD required."**

## What these numbers actually tell you

Four things stand out, and all four are read straight off the official notes rather than inferred.

**Both tiers are 1080p targets.** Neither the minimum nor the recommended row promises 1440p or 4K. Recommended buys you **Medium settings at 1080p and 60fps** — not a higher resolution. If you want to run above 1080p, you are already past the published guidance and into untested territory until launch benchmarks exist.

**Both tiers assume upscaling, in Performance mode.** The minimum row expects DLSS or FSR1; the recommended row expects DLSS or FSR3. The parenthetical **"(Performance)"** is doing real work here — that is the aggressive upscaling preset, not Quality or Balanced. So the quoted 40fps and 60fps figures are already upscaled numbers, not native ones.

**An SSD is mandatory, not advisory.** Both notes say "SSD required," and Steam separately lists **Gamepad Recommended** among the store categories. A mechanical drive is not a supported configuration.

**16 GB RAM is the floor and the ceiling.** The minimum and recommended rows are identical at 16 GB. The scaling Game Freak expects is on the **GPU** (8 GB → 12 GB VRAM) and the **CPU** (i5-8400 → i7-12700K), not on system memory.

## The upscaling caveat worth knowing

The minimum note names **DLSS (RTX 20-50 Series)** in the same breath as a **GTX 1070**, and the GTX 1070 does not support DLSS — it predates the RTX tensor-core hardware DLSS needs. Read carefully, the note is listing two separate routes to the same target: DLSS if you have an RTX card, **FSR1** if you are on the GTX 1070 / RX 580 class of hardware. It is not claiming a GTX 1070 can run DLSS.

That matters practically: if you are on a GTX 1070 or RX 580, plan on **FSR1 Performance at 1080p Low, targeting 40fps**. That is the official expectation for that hardware, and it is a modest one.

## Storage, platform and features

- **45 GB** of available space, on an SSD.
- **Windows only on Steam.** The Steam listing reports Windows support and does **not** list macOS or Linux. There is no official Steam Deck Verified rating published as of 3 August 2026.
- Steam lists **Single-player**, **Steam Achievements**, **Steam Cloud**, **Full controller support** (with DualShock and DualSense support noted), **Adjustable Difficulty**, **Custom Volume Controls**, **Subtitle Options**, **Stereo Sound**, and **Family Sharing**.
- **Languages:** full audio in **English and Japanese**, plus text support for French, German, Spanish (Spain), Korean, Portuguese (Brazil), Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Italian, and Spanish (Latin America).

## What the published specifications still do not answer

The official targets do not promise native-resolution performance, a 1440p or 4K frame rate, or performance on hardware outside the named tiers. They also do not establish the result of a particular driver, display, Steam Deck, or handheld configuration. Those are separate tests, not missing lines in the requirements table.
