**Beast of Reincarnation** is built around two protagonists rather than a large party. Emma carries the sword and traversal tools, while Koo supplies the bloom arts and status pressure that make the hybrid combat system work.

## Emma: sword, movement and positioning

Emma is the human side of the combat loop. Her sword handles normal attacks, parries, blade arts and ripostes. Her hair also provides the traversal abilities that turn vertical spaces and broken bridges into routes: **Lift** reaches higher ledges, while **Extend** creates a temporary bridge across gaps.

For a first run, prioritize a full dodge roll and one dependable blade art before chasing a complicated damage setup. Emma is safest when she can parry a readable attack, spend the resulting entanglement, then leave the danger zone before the next enemy commits.

## Koo: bloom arts and elemental answers

Koo is not a passive companion. His bloom arts consume florescence points and can apply elemental pressure, Bind, healing or stagger depending on the art. The menu slows time, but it does not turn every decision into a full pause, so open it after creating distance or after a successful parry.

The useful rule is to read the weakness indicator beside the target's health bar. Match Koo's art to the displayed weakness instead of assigning one element as a permanent best choice. A successful bloom-art QTE can also restore health, making Koo part of Emma's recovery plan.

## How the pair fits together

1. Emma reads the enemy's attack and parries or dodges.
2. The parry builds the resources that unlock Koo's strongest responses.
3. Koo applies the matching art or Bind while Emma takes the safe punish window.
4. Emma returns to spacing instead of extending a combo into a red attack.

The [first-hours build guide](/guides/first-hours-build-and-combat/) explains this loop in a practical order. For skill priorities, use the [Emma skill tree](/skills/emma-skill-tree/) and [Koo Bloom Arts guide](/guides/koo-bloom-arts-qte-guide/).

## What is not a character roster

Emma and Koo are the confirmed central pair. This page does not invent recruitable party members, alternate playable characters or companion stat growth. The released game has skill trees, weapons and spirit stones, but those progression systems should not be mistaken for a roster screen.
