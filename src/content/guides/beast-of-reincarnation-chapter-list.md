## How long is the story?

Current hands-on coverage places a normal main-story run around **20–30 hours**, with a more completion-focused run around **30–40 hours**. Difficulty, exploration, optional bosses and time spent learning the combat loop can move the number substantially. It is an estimate, not a timer enforced by the game.

## What to prepare between chapters

Use the end of a chapter to check three things: whether Emma's next traversal ability is available, whether the Cleanse Walker has enough upgrade materials, and whether the next boss route has a nearby campsite. The [first-hours guide](/guides/first-hours-build-and-combat/) and [upgrade materials guide](/guides/cleanse-walker-upgrade-materials-guide/) cover those systems without turning the chapter list into a fabricated 100% route.
