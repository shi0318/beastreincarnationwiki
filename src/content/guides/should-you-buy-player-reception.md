Beast of Reincarnation launched on **4 August 2026** to a **Mixed** rating on Steam — and if you're reading reviews trying to decide whether to buy, that split is genuinely confusing, because the same game is getting "labour of love" praise and refund threads on the same page. This guide sorts the launch-week reaction into what's actually a dealbreaker and what isn't, drawn from players in the [official Steam forums](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2001760/discussions/).

We haven't reviewed the game ourselves; this is a digest of what players said, attributed throughout. Review counts move daily — check the [Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2001760/) for the live number rather than trusting any snapshot.

## The state of reception

As of launch day the Steam user rating sat at **Mixed**, with a bit over half of reviews positive out of roughly a thousand — and climbing through the day as more people got past the rocky first hour. The pattern in the forums matches: **strong reactions in both directions**, with a large middle group who like the game but are frustrated by its technical state and want patches.

The most common arc, seen repeatedly, is a player threatening to refund in their first hour and then cancelling the refund:

> "I cancelled my refund, still on the fence. I love the music and the idea… I'm sure they will do some QOL updates probably soon. I was just super surprised with the weak points right out the gate."

## What players consistently praise

These come up again and again, even in negative posts:

- **The world, art and atmosphere.** The post-apocalyptic Japan setting, the nature-reclaims-machines aesthetic, and the character designs for Emma and Koo are near-universally liked. Comparisons to **NieR: Automata** and **Stellar Blade** recur.
- **The music and voice acting.** "The music is amazing, the voice acting is awesome" is a sentiment even refund-leaning players repeat. (One caveat below.)
- **Combat once it opens up.** Players who push past the first hour report the combat "gets really fun once you unlock more skills and combo finishers," with a fast, agile feel some compare to **Nioh**. The parry is forgiving — see our [difficulty and combat guide](/guides/difficulty-and-combat-feel/).
- **Koo, the dog.** The companion is a big part of the game's charm for most players, even the ones who think he over-helps during exploration.
- **It runs well on capable hardware.** Plenty of players report smooth, high-framerate play — the performance complaints are real but not universal.

## What makes people refund

The negatives cluster into two buckets. The first is **technical/PC polish**, and it's the loudest:

- **No FOV slider** (one player reported motion sickness), a **too-close camera** in crowded fights, **film grain and chromatic aberration** with no clean in-menu toggle, the **upscaler resetting to FSR every launch**, **pop-in / LOD** issues, and **no native ultrawide**. Our [PC performance fixes guide](/guides/pc-performance-settings-fixes/) covers the workarounds players found for most of these.
- These are widely described as "mostly quick fixes," which is why so many players are choosing to **wait for patches** rather than refund outright.

The second bucket is **design**, and it's more of a taste question:

- **Repetitive boss lead-ins.** A 12-hour player's summary: "before almost every boss, you're doing the same thing: find keys, activate batteries, solve a puzzle, ring a bell… it gets repetitive fast."
- **Predictable bosses.** "Bosses are too easy, even on Hard Mode. They hit hard, but their moves are very predictable." Many bosses follow a fight-flee-refight structure that adds only one or two new attacks the second time.
- **Limited biome variety** — a lot of ruined cities, towns and caves.
- **One music track** complaint from a couple of players (in tension with the "music is amazing" praise — the composition is liked, the *variety* during fights is questioned).
- **A rough, tutorial-light opening** that "throws you in without explaining anything" and stiff out-of-combat movement.

## How long is it, and is there replay value?

Players cite roughly **30–40 hours** to beat, longer if you explore. On replay value the picture from discussion is modest: exploration is "decent" but **don't expect secret bosses** — most optional bosses are just tougher versions of regular enemies. It's a **single-player, story-driven** game with no co-op or online features, so treat it as a one-good-playthrough experience rather than something with heavy endgame.

## Editions and the pre-order bonus confusion

A very common launch-day question, so it's worth clearing up:

- **Standard vs Deluxe.** Buying now, the **Deluxe upgrade includes everything**, including the former pre-order items. The Deluxe upgrade is sold as a standalone **$9.99 DLC** (App 4146830), so standard buyers can add it later — it's cosmetics, Emma's "Big Dipper" sword, seedlings and **100,000 Amber**, no story content.
- **The pre-order bonus is a separate thing** — a Koo skin (the brown/"Yellow Dog" Shiba) plus **30,000 Amber**. Per a detailed player breakdown, the Deluxe edition includes the skin and its own 100k Amber but **not** that specific 30k pre-order bonus on top.
- **"Where's my bonus?"** Multiple players (both pre-order and Deluxe) reported the items not appearing. Two things: the content unlocks **after the tutorial**, claimed at the **rest area / bed in your base**, not immediately; and several players believe the **pre-order DLC wasn't correctly attached to the standard edition** at launch, so some bonuses simply hadn't activated — a launch bug, not a scam. If yours is missing, that's why.

## Buy now or wait?

The honest read from launch-week players:

- **Buy now if:** you have a capable PC (or play on **Game Pass**, where it's included day one and the buy-in risk is zero), you want the world/music/combat and can tolerate rough edges, and you're willing to apply a couple of the community fixes. Many players are having a genuinely good time.
- **Wait if:** you're on a **Steam Deck** (not Verified yet — see [performance guide](/guides/pc-performance-settings-fixes/)), you need **ultrawide** or a **FOV slider**, or you want challenging, varied bosses. The technical issues are the kind that patch well, so waiting a few weeks is a reasonable play.
- **The safety net:** keep your first session **under 2 hours** if you buy on Steam, so a refund stays on the table. That's the exact advice players gave each other.
