**Koo** is the partner, not a second health bar you can hide behind. Use the wolf for the opening burst and for crowds. Put **Emma** back on the stick when a named Nushi starts a parry string.

Koo's Bloom Arts and the QTE window are the payoff for building the bond. They are not a replacement for Emma's parry. A fight that needs a red-attack dodge still needs Emma's dodge.

## A simple switch

- Horde or a long approach: open on Koo so the first wave does not reach Emma's guard. See [Koo's combat role](/guides/beast-of-reincarnation-koo-combat-role/).
- A single elite or a Nushi with a published weakness: Emma, the matching element, and the [weakness chart](/guides/beast-of-reincarnation-boss-weakness-chart/).
- A QTE prompt: do the prompt. Missing it is not a damage race you can mash through. The [Bloom Arts page](/guides/koo-bloom-arts-qte-guide/) is that input.

Do not spend the whole skill tree on Koo before Emma can roll. The early dodge unlock still decides whether a red attack ends the attempt.
