There are two named ending achievements in the released Steam record: **Life After the End** and **The End of Reincarnation**. Both were sitting at **24.2% global unlock** when the achievement table was checked on September 1, 2026. The percentage is a snapshot, not a claim that the endings are equally difficult or equally secret.

## What the achievement record tells you

The achievement list confirms that two ending states are tracked separately. It does not publish the decision conditions, the order in which the endings appear, or whether one requires a particular optional activity. The full completion achievement, **Duty Fulfilled**, was at 2.7% on the same snapshot.

That makes the safest completion plan simple: keep a manual save before a major final decision, finish the story once, and check the achievement panel before assuming every ending condition is understood.

## Story order and optional content

The main story has 13 chapters followed by the Bearing Fruit epilogue. Optional exploration, Nushi fights, shrines and upgrade materials can be handled around that progression. The [chapter list](/guides/beast-of-reincarnation-chapter-list/) gives the order without publishing every story beat.

For a completion run, combine the [achievements guide](/guides/achievements-guide/) with the [valuables and collectibles guide](/guides/valuables-collectibles-guide/). Do not treat the two ending names as proof of two simple dialogue choices; the public list does not explain the trigger.

## Spoiler boundary

This page deliberately does not state the ending scenes, reveal the Beast's final explanation or assign hidden achievements to individual chapters. That information should only be added after a reproducible released-build record exists.
