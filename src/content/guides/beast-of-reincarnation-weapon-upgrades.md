Weapon progression in **Beast of Reincarnation** is tied to the wider Cleanse Walker and skill-tree loop. The safest early decision is to improve the weapon and ammunition you can use consistently, not to chase an unsupported tier list.

## The upgrade categories

The current weapon record separates Emma's named blades, the **Spring Thunder Crossbow**, arrows and bolts. The [weapons index](/weapons/) keeps those categories separate because ammunition and weapon effects answer different questions.

- **Blade:** your reliable close-range tool and the weapon used for parries, blade arts and ripostes.
- **Crossbow:** a ranged option for creating pressure before an enemy reaches Emma.
- **Arrows and bolts:** ammunition choices that should be matched to the encounter rather than spent on every ordinary target.
- **Walker upgrades:** capacity and level improvements that open the equipment and material loop.

## A sensible upgrade order

1. Upgrade the blade you can parry and recover with reliably.
2. Keep one ranged option supplied for bosses and enemies that punish close contact.
3. Improve Cleanse Walker capacity and level when the equipment screen blocks the next upgrade.
4. Add elemental or mod coverage after you know which weaknesses your current route actually presents.

Generator Devices, Electronic Brains and Memory Chips are the confirmed material names used in the Walker progression notes. The [Cleanse Walker guide](/guides/cleanse-walker-upgrade-materials-guide/) explains the gate without inventing a complete drop map.

## What “best weapon” does not prove

The released data does not establish a universal damage ranking, frame table or identical upgrade path on every difficulty. A weapon with a larger displayed effect can still be a poor choice if its recovery is unsafe for your timing. Test one change at a time against the same enemy and record the build before comparing results.
