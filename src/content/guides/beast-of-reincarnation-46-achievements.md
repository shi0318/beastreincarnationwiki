Steam App 2001760 lists **46 achievements**. That is the completion ceiling on the October 16, 2026 storefront. It is not a chapter-by-chapter missable table, and it is not a reason to restart before **v1.1.01** is installed.

Boss achievements still follow the named Nushi. Collectible achievements still follow the hubs. The storefront count does not add a 47th task.

## How to clear toward 46

- Fight pages and the [weakness chart](/guides/beast-of-reincarnation-boss-weakness-chart/) cover the named encounters. Unlock rates on Steam are the official half. Attack lists are walkthrough notes.
- [Collectibles](/collectibles/) split valuables, locked treasures, spirit stones, seedlings, and the Cleanse Walker. Do not merge those into one chest route.
- **Hasten to the Capital** can skip regions. If you care about the list, take the long route first. The skip is a replay tool, not a completion tool. See [Hasten to the Capital](/guides/beast-of-reincarnation-hasten-to-the-capital/).

If a pop fails after v1.1.01, retest the same save before you assume the achievement was removed.
