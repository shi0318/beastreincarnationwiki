Patch **v1.1.01** (9 October 2026, the eighth update since launch) added a story shortcut. After you clear **The Great Wall Terraces**, the game now asks whether to **Hasten to the Capital** or take the **Mountain Route** as normal. Hastening skips three whole zones.

## What the choice skips

| Zone | Chapter | What you miss if you hasten |
|---|---|---|
| Pilgrim's Zone | 4 – Stems | The two **Taurus** fights, 11 campsites, 7 shrines, the Sunrise Sword |
| Matsukawa Colony Zone | 5 – Branching Paths | 12 campsites, 9 shrines, **10 documents**, Resonance Sword, Hazebringer |
| Fuji Frontier Zone | 6 – Necrosis | 9 campsites, 5 shrines, 4 Keeper Golems, Shark Cutter, the Explosive Arrow manual |

## The achievement catch

The patch notes are explicit: if you choose Hasten to the Capital on a **New Game** run, the achievements tied to those three zones cannot be earned on that run. They are still available on **New Game+** if you pick the **Mountain Route** there. Taurus's End (34.8% on Steam) is the obvious one, but shrine, document and collection achievements also depend on these zones.

## Who should hasten

- **NG+ players** who already have the zone achievements and want to reach the Capital quickly.
- **Story-first players** who found the middle chapters repetitive – the most common complaint in Mixed reviews.

## Who should take the Mountain Route

- Anyone going for 100% on a first run.
- Anyone who wants the gear in these zones: Sunrise Sword (Flame), Hazebringer (Acid), Shark Cutter (crit) and Explosive Arrows (Erymanth's weakness).
- Players who are under-levelled – the Capital fights (Erymanth at 47, Kunai and Kirin at 49) assume you have the experience from these zones.

## The rest of v1.1.01

- A **Field of View** slider in Options.
- A setting to show or hide the cutscene skip prompt.
- Fixes for subtitles staying on screen after a skip, soft locks from rapid skipping, odd camera angles after skips, Entanglement Overdrive toggling lock-on, and Koo using items on staggered enemies.
- The Explosive Arrow manual from a Keeper Golem can now be obtained in New Game+.

Fictions also said the next patch, planned for around **19 October**, will be the last in this phase of frequent post-launch updates.
