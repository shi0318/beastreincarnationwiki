Patch **v1.1.01**, dated **9 October 2026** and described as the eighth update since launch, added a story shortcut. After you clear **The Great Wall Terraces**, the game asks whether to **Hasten to the Capital** or take the **Mountain Route**. Hastening skips three whole zones. It does not complete them, and it does not move their chests into the Capital.

The three zones are **Pilgrim's Zone**, **Matsukawa Colony Zone**, and **Fuji Frontier Zone**. On a first file those are chapters, gear, and achievements, not empty road. On **October 20, 2026** the Steam listing was still **$59.99** with no discount, and reviews were Mixed: **3,780** positive out of **6,399**. A skip that cuts the middle of the game is one reason that score exists. It is also the fastest way to lock yourself out of the pops those zones own.

## What the choice skips

| Zone | Chapter | What you miss if you hasten |
|---|---|---|
| Pilgrim's Zone | 4 – Stems | The two **Taurus** fights, 11 campsites, 7 shrines, the Sunrise Sword |
| Matsukawa Colony Zone | 5 – Branching Paths | 12 campsites, 9 shrines, **10 documents**, Resonance Sword, Hazebringer |
| Fuji Frontier Zone | 6 – Necrosis | 9 campsites, 5 shrines, 4 Keeper Golems, Shark Cutter, the Explosive Arrow manual |

That table is the decision. Pilgrim's Zone is not a corridor. It is two Taurus fights, eleven campsites, seven shrines, and the Sunrise Sword. Matsukawa is twelve campsites, nine shrines, ten documents, the Resonance Sword, and Hazebringer. Fuji Frontier is nine campsites, five shrines, four Keeper Golems, the Shark Cutter, and the Explosive Arrow manual. Hastening does not drop those items in the Capital as a consolation prize. It leaves them in the zones you did not walk.

Count the campsites if the "I will grab it later" plan is tempting. Eleven plus twelve plus nine is thirty-two campsites, plus twenty-one shrines, plus the documents and the four Keeper Golems. That is not a single missed chest. It is the middle third of the map. A story-first player can accept that. A first-run collector cannot, and the patch notes already say so.

## The achievement catch

The patch notes are explicit. If you choose Hasten to the Capital on a **New Game** run, the achievements tied to those three zones cannot be earned on that run. They are still available on **New Game+** if you pick the **Mountain Route** there. Taurus's End, listed around **34.8%** on Steam when this page was first checked, is the obvious one. Shrine, document, and collection achievements that live in these zones follow the same rule.

Steam lists **46** achievements on App **2001760**. On October 20 the global board still showed an early game almost everyone touches and a late game most people do not. **ACH_PURIFIER** was **94.6%**. **ACH_ENCAMPMENT** was **92.5%**. **ACH_GROWTH_OF_IMPURITY** was **83.0%**. **ACH_BEAST_OF_REINCARNATION** and **ACH_LIVE** were both **27.2%**. **ACH_BEST_BUDDY** was **7.3%**, **ACH_THE_ULTIMATE_VESSEL** **7.4%**, and **ACH_FAMILY_DINING_TABLE** **7.7%**. Hastening will not create those rare pops. It will block the mid-game ones on the file you skip.

The recovery path is not a reload. It is a later New Game+ file that takes the Mountain Route. If you do not intend to play that second file, the skipped achievements are gone for the way you actually play. Read the prompt with that in mind. "Hasten" sounds like a convenience. On a New Game file it is a lock.

## Who should hasten

NG+ players who already have the zone achievements are the player the shortcut was written for. The notes say the option exists so New Game+ runs can move faster, and so story-focused players can reach the Capital without walking Pilgrim's Zone, Matsukawa, and Fuji Frontier again. If the swords in the table are already in a previous file, and the Taurus achievement has already popped, hastening is a time tool. Use it.

Story-first players on a first file can use it too, if they have already decided the middle chapters are the part they do not want. Mixed reviews have been saying the loop repeats since August. The skip is the studio's answer. Take it only after you accept the gear in the table and the achievement lock. Do not take it to "see the Capital and then come back." The notes do not describe a return door that restores the blocked pops.

## Who should take the Mountain Route

Anyone going for a full clear on a first run should walk. Anyone who wants the named gear should walk. The Sunrise Sword is the flame weapon in Pilgrim's Zone. Hazebringer is the acid weapon in Matsukawa, next to the Resonance Sword. The Shark Cutter is the crit weapon in Fuji Frontier, and the Explosive Arrow manual is there too. v1.1.01 separately fixed the Explosive Arrow manual from a Keeper Golem so it can be obtained in New Game+. That fix does not help a New Game file that never entered the zone.

Players who are under-levelled should also walk. The Capital fights assume the experience from these zones. Erymanth is the fight at **47**. Kunai and Kirin are at **49**. Skipping three chapters and then meeting those numbers is how a story shortcut becomes a wall. If the terraces already felt thin, the Mountain Route is the level band. Hastening is the opposite of a grind. It is also the opposite of preparation.

A third group should walk even if they do not care about 100%: anyone using the skip to dodge a quest bug. A bypass does not complete a stuck objective. It leaves the zone. If Fuji Frontier is the bug, hastening does not resolve Fuji Frontier. It abandons it. Fix or abandon the quest on purpose. Do not confuse the two.

## How to stand at the door

Clear The Great Wall Terraces first. The prompt is not available before that clear. When it appears, treat the two labels as a fork.

1. Open the achievement list and the inventory before you confirm.
2. If Taurus's End, the shrine pops, or the document pops from these zones are still open on a New Game file, choose the Mountain Route.
3. If the Sunrise Sword, Hazebringer, Shark Cutter, or Explosive Arrow manual is still a reason you are playing, choose the Mountain Route.
4. If this is New Game+ and those items and pops are already done, Hasten to the Capital.
5. If you hasten anyway, do not reload the same file and expect the blocked achievements to appear after the Capital. The notes say they cannot be earned on that New Game run.

Exploring one of the three zones and then returning to the prompt is still a skip if you confirm Hasten. Partial progress in a zone you then bypass does not satisfy the achievement rule the patch printed. Finish the zone or leave it. Do not half-clear it and call that a Mountain Route.

## The rest of v1.1.01, so you do not skip for the wrong reason

The same patch added a **Field of View** slider in Options and a setting to show or hide the cutscene skip prompt. It fixed subtitles staying on screen after a skip, soft locks from rapid skipping, odd camera angles after skips, Entanglement Overdrive toggling lock-on, and Koo using items on staggered enemies. Those are menu and combat fixes. They are not locked behind the Mountain Route, and they are not a reason to hasten.

[The field-of-view page](/guides/beast-of-reincarnation-fov-and-skip-prompts/) is the place for the camera. If the frame is too close, move the slider. Do not skip eleven campsites because a subtitle stuck in August. That subtitle bug is on the fixed list.

Fictions also said the next patch, planned for around **19 October**, would be the last in this phase of frequent post-launch updates. A route choice on October 20 is a choice on the build you have. Do not hasten because you expect a later patch to mail you the Sunrise Sword. The closing note is about update cadence, not about restoring skipped zones. The longer read is [the support-cycle page](/guides/beast-of-reincarnation-support-is-ending/).
