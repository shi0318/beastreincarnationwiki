The current Steam Community interactive-map guide says that seedlings found across the zones are brought back to the Cleanse Walker, where the crops feed its kitchen and cooking recipes provide buffs. It also separates the **Fertilized Egg** from ordinary food ingredients, saying that the egg can be hatched inside the Walker. The official Steam store page does not publish a complete recipe list, ingredient quantities, or buff durations, so this is a systems guide rather than a fabricated cookbook.

## The source-backed cooking loop

1. Collect seedling types while exploring a region.
2. Track each type separately for the [Green Thumb checklist](/guides/seedlings-crops-guide/).
3. Return the seedlings to the Cleanse Walker rather than treating every crop as a combat drop.
4. Check what the kitchen makes available in your version before spending rare ingredients.
5. Record the recipe, ingredients, buff, and duration in your own notes if you want a versioned database.

The map's crop filters include examples such as Strawberry, Apple, Rice, Soy, Tomato, Potato, Carrot, Lettuce, Wheat, and Life Seedling. That is useful for planning a search, but the map author can add new filters independently of the game. Use the [interactive map guide](/guides/interactive-map-guide/) to see its current region coverage.

## What this guide will not invent

There is no first-party Steam table here for “best food,” exact recipe unlocks, or universal buff stacking. A screenshot of one player's kitchen is not enough to establish a full list. Until each recipe is checked in a shipped build, leave unknown fields blank and do not use the food system as a reason to publish fake build rankings.
