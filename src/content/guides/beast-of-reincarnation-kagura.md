**Kagura** is a withdrawn young woman living in one of the fortified colonies Emma passes through. She becomes one of the "unlikely allies" of the story and spends time aboard the Cleanse Walker, where some of the quieter character scenes take place.

## At a glance

| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Role | Colony resident turned ally |
| Where | A fortified colony, later aboard the Cleanse Walker |
| Voice | Not published on the [voice-cast page](/guides/voice-cast-and-previews/). |

## Story role

Kagura contrasts with Emma: Emma has lost her memories and emotions to the blight, while Kagura is sheltered and cautious. Their conversations on the Walker are part of the affinity-building scenes that patch 1.0.7 limited to once per day.

## Gameplay notes

- Patch 1.0.7 fixed a bug where pressing the interact button during the story event where you talk to Kagura on the Walker could trigger the wrong input when interact was mapped to Square/X.
- Most of her scenes can be skipped since patches 1.0.11 and 1.0.12 if you are replaying for combat.
