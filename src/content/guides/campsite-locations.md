Steam gives campsites two clear achievement goals: **First Fire** unlocks when you discover your first campsite, and **Happy Camper** asks you to discover every campsite. Steam does not publish a complete location list on the store page. The current Steam Community map guide provides a **Camp** filter for its indexed zones, so it is the most useful live source for a cleanup run while still being player-authored.

## A practical campsite route

1. Discover the first campsite naturally before using a full map sweep.
2. On the [Steam interactive map](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3778455109), select the region you are currently exploring.
3. Turn on only the **Camp** filter and compare each marker with an in-game landmark.
4. Record the campsite name or nearby landmark in your own checklist after interacting with it.
5. Recheck the map after a game update; the author says the map is updated as filters and pins are added.

The same Steam map guide reports that resting at a campsite heals Emma and respawns the zone, and that a death restarts the player from the last campsite without an Amber, material, or experience loss. Those are community-guide observations, not a guarantee from the store listing; if your build behaves differently, trust the in-game result and record the version.

For the full filter workflow, open the [interactive map guide](/guides/interactive-map-guide/) before starting a region-wide cleanup.

## Campsites are not shrines

The first shrine unlocks a different Steam achievement, **Place of Return**. Do not combine shrine markers with campsite markers when checking **Happy Camper**. Use the separate [Wayside Shrine guide](/guides/wayside-shrines-guide/) for that objective, and the [achievement guide](/guides/achievements-guide/) for the wider completion list.
