**Mikoto** is the reported reset for Amber spent on skills. You meet him in the Ancient Civilization Zone after the first Lacerta fight. Patch 1.0.11 also made flower-node skills resettable, so Koo's Bloom Arts can be reworked without a new file.

Reset before you walk into the next Nushi. A respec after the first phase does not rewind the attempt.

## Order

1. Talk to Mikoto. Do not assume a menu in the Cleanse Walker does this. [Mikoto](/guides/beast-of-reincarnation-mikoto/).
2. Pull the points off the tree you are not using. Emma's dodge and the parry loop stay. A pure Koo tree does not dodge red attacks for you.
3. Equip the element the next boss actually wants. [Chart](/guides/beast-of-reincarnation-boss-weakness-chart/).
4. If you are farming Amber to afford the reset, use the [Amber farm](/guides/beast-of-reincarnation-amber-farming/) before the door, not during it.
