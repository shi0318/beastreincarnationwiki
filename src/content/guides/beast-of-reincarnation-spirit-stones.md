Spirit Stones are part of the released game's build customization, alongside skill trees, gear and weapon choices. They are best understood as a way to support a playstyle rather than a reason to copy a single damage setup.

## Three useful build directions

**Parry and counter:** supports a player who is comfortable waiting for readable normal attacks. It pairs naturally with entanglement spending and riposte windows.

**Ranged and control:** keeps distance with the crossbow, ammunition and Koo's Bind or elemental pressure. It is useful when an encounter punishes standing at the target's feet.

**Stealth and exploration:** favors safe openings, air assassinations and movement through optional routes before a fight begins. It is less about a single high number and more about entering an arena on your terms.

## How to compare stones

Change one stone at a time and keep the enemy, difficulty and attack input constant. Record whether the result changed normal damage, stagger pressure, FP use, entanglement recovery or survivability. If a description only says that an effect is stronger, do not turn it into an exact percentage without a reproducible test.

The [progression and builds guide](/guides/progression-and-builds/) is the broad reference. The [ranged, stealth and aggressive builds guide](/guides/ranged-stealth-aggressive-builds/) compares the three playstyle goals without calling one of them universally best.

## What remains version-sensitive

Patch notes can alter effect wording, costs or interactions. Save the patch number with every comparison, and recheck a stone after a combat rebalance. A “best Spirit Stone” answer without a build date is likely to age badly.
