## What players are searching for after the update

The useful answer to “what did the Beast of Reincarnation update fix?” is narrower than a general review. Version 1.0.7 changes camera behaviour, text size, controls, combat balance, and several PC settings or interaction bugs. It does not promise that every report from launch has been resolved.

## Confirmed v1.0.7 fixes and adjustments

The official Steam announcement lists these changes:

- Camera adjustments both in and out of combat, with more camera work planned.
- Text-size adjustments throughout the game, with further work planned.
- Repeated affinity conversations are limited to once per day in cases where they could repeat in sequence.
- The default cutscene setting changes from Cinematic to Performance. Cinematic remains available as an option.
- The Interact button can now be remapped in Settings.
- The parry window was adjusted when Emma is taking damage.
- Boss balance changes apply across all difficulty levels, and Normal enemy encounters were also adjusted.
- Button-icon type changes now save correctly.
- Upscaling settings no longer reset when the game restarts.
- Changing V-Sync no longer changes the selected resolution.
- The sixth golem in Subcity 01 is easier to find.
- Emma should no longer become unable to interact after a Bloom Art is cancelled.

## PC checklist after installing v1.0.7

1. Restart Steam and wait for the update to finish before judging an old issue.
2. Open the graphics menu once and confirm the selected resolution and upscaling option. The patch addresses a reset bug, but your actual setting is still the best baseline for troubleshooting.
3. Test the Interact binding and button-icon style in a safe area if you use a custom control layout.
4. If a boss or Normal encounter feels different, record the difficulty and the exact enemy or boss before comparing it with an older clip.
5. If the problem remains, report the build/version, hardware where relevant, and a reproducible route instead of assuming v1.0.7 covers it.

The [PC performance and settings guide](/guides/pc-performance-settings-fixes/) is the companion page for graphics troubleshooting. It should be read alongside this update page because the patch notes describe fixes, not a universal best-settings preset.

## What is planned, not delivered by this patch

The same official announcement mentions further story-pacing and cutscene improvements, plus planned DLSS 4/4.5 (L/M) and FSR4 support. Those are future plans in the developer post. They are not features that v1.0.7 is confirmed to have delivered, so do not treat them as current PC settings.

**They have since shipped, in later patches.** DLSS 4/4.5 and FSR 4 arrived in **v1.0.8**; skippable cutscenes and frame generation arrived in **v1.0.9**; the post-processing toggles arrived in **v1.0.10**, which is the current version. If you are troubleshooting today rather than reading a record of the first patch, start from the [patch notes index](/guides/patch-notes/) or the [v1.0.10 notes](/guides/beast-of-reincarnation-update-1-0-10/).
