Beast of Reincarnation is an action-RPG, and the "RPG" half is real: Game Freak has confirmed several distinct progression systems, reinforced in the July Combat Overview Trailer. This page collects what is officially stated about building Emma and Koo. It is sourced from official materials, so it is not one of our speculative previews.

## The confirmed systems

Official copy names three progression pillars:

- **Skill trees** — Emma and Koo grow along developable paths rather than a single linear level track.
- **Gear** — equipment that shapes Emma's capabilities.
- **Spirit stones** — a distinct customisation layer called out separately from gear.

Together these are the pieces the store page points to when it says you can define **how** you fight, not just how strong you are.

## Capturing Boss powers

The detail worth watching most closely: a core goal is to **defeat Bosses and capture their powers**. That is progression tied to **encounters**, not only to a menu — beating a Boss gives you something that changes what you can do next, feeding back into your build. It is also the thread that leads to the final confrontation with the **Beast of Reincarnation** itself.

How captured powers slot into the skill trees, gear, or spirit-stone systems has not been detailed, so treat the *mechanism* as unconfirmed even though the *goal* is official.

## Three loadout directions

Game Freak has repeatedly highlighted three broad playstyles the systems support:

| Direction | The fantasy |
| --- | --- |
| **Ranged** | Fighting at distance, controlling space before enemies close |
| **Stealthy** | Avoiding detection and picking engagements |
| **Aggressive** | Pressuring enemies up close with Emma's sword and Koo's techniques |

Naming three archetypes in launch marketing is a strong signal that build variety is a genuine pillar, not a bullet point. What is **not** published in the official material tracked here: the skill-tree layout, how spirit stones are acquired or slotted, whether respeccing is free, and how gear rarity works.

## Amber, the in-game currency

Pre-order material confirms **Amber** as the in-game currency (the Deluxe edition grants 100,000 of it, the standard edition 30,000). Its exact use — buying gear, upgrades, or consumables — is not spelled out yet, but its existence tells you there is an economy layer to manage alongside the skill and gear systems.

## Why we are not publishing a tier list

Because the official material tracked here does not publish the actual numbers, we are not ranking builds or skills from marketing copy. A reliable build guide needs the tree layout, acquisition routes, and how captured Boss powers interact; those details should be backed by a current-build record rather than inferred.
