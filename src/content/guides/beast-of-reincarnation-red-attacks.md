A red glow on an enemy attack is the tell that **parry and guard will not answer it**. Dodge. Then go back to the normal loop. Mixing those two inputs is how a readable fight becomes a cartwheel.

Parry and guard share one button. A clean parry is what opens Blade Arts and the Flow window. A red attack is outside that button. If you hold guard because the last three swings were parryable, the red one still lands.

## What to do when the glow starts

1. Stop the parry input. Do not mash the shared guard button through the glow.
2. Dodge off the line. Unlock the roll early if you are still on the short step — the [controls page](/guides/parry-guard-dodge-controls-guide/) is the drill.
3. After the red swing recovers, parry the next normal string. That is the damage window, not the glow itself.

Boss pages name which swings are the red ones. Rangifer's red-glow escape is on the [Rangifer fight](/guides/nushi-boss-fight/). Do not copy that escape onto Lacerta or Kunai. Their red swings are different animations.

## What this page will not invent

Frame data, a perfect-dodge reward table, and a claim that every red attack is a one-hit kill. The rule that is stable is the input split: glow means dodge, everything else is the parry button.
