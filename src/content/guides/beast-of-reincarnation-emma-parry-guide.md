The parry is the heart of Beast of Reincarnation. Every successful parry builds **Flow**, the resource Koo spends on Bloom Arts and Nushi skills – so a player who dodges everything starves half their kit.

## How the parry works

- Press guard as the strike lands. A clean parry deals stagger, restores some HP and Flow, and opens a punish.
- **Story** difficulty widens the window. No achievements are locked behind difficulty.
- Patch **v1.0.7** adjusted the parry window when Emma is already taking damage – the moment most failed parries happen.
- Filling an enemy's down gauge opens a takedown that does far more than chip damage.

## What to parry and what not to

| Parry | Do not parry |
|---|---|
| Rangifer's normal antler swing | Rangifer's red four-sweep combo – give space |
| Lacerta's three-bite Mouth Snap and tail swipe | Lacerta's underground lunge |
| Taurus's triple horn swing | Taurus's charge – use Bind |
| Erymanth's red 5-hit tusk combo | Erymanth's red-flash charge – never |
| Kunai's melee strings | Kirin's missiles – one-hit kills |
| Shidou's sword and branch swings | Shidou's vertical tree slam – perfect-dodge |

The pattern: **red glow can mean either**, so learn each boss's tell rather than reacting to colour alone.

## Practising

Heavy Golems in Ogouchi (levels 6–9) are slow and readable – ideal for timing. Then try the Rangifer crater fight without dodging.

## Gear and skills that help

- **Purifier Blade** (starting sword): taking damage briefly reduces Emma's stagger.
- **Purity** heals a share of damage dealt after a parry punish.
- Emma's skill tree (Giant Tree Budburst achievement) unlocks Blade Arts and Finishers that turn parries into big damage.
