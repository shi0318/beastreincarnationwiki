The parry is why people who like the combat stay. It is also why people who do not like the combat leave. Patch 1.0.7 adjusted the window while Emma is taking damage, which is the moment most failed parries happen.

## When to parry

Use it on a horizontal swing you can see start. The click is the feedback. If you are waiting for a health-bar flash, you are late.

Do not parry:

- A downward headbutt. Players called this out after it showed up on some bosses. It comes from above the camera. Roll or step. The parry hitbox is not where your eyes are.
- A grab with no weapon trail. Parry is for a strike, not a command grab.
- The first hit of a combo if you have not seen that Nushi before. Eat the chip on Normal, learn the tell, parry the repeat.

Koo is not your parry button. The wolf opens space and starts fights. Emma answers the swing. If the mapping feels late, that is a reported feel issue, not a setting you missed. 1.1.0 did not add an input-buffer slider.
