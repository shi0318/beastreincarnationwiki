The Cleanse Walker is the loadout screen. **Brad** is the pilot and the late shop. If a merchant blade such as the Bell Sword or Winterwake was missed, his stock is the retry, not a chapter restart. See [Brad](/guides/beast-of-reincarnation-brad/).

A Nushi attempt that starts with the wrong element is a longer fight, not a harder one. Change the blade here.

## Four checks

- **Element.** Fire for Rangifer, shock for Lacerta, acid for Taurus and Ursa, explosive for Erymanth, poison for the duo fights. The final fight has no element. [Chart](/guides/beast-of-reincarnation-boss-weakness-chart/).
- **Dodge.** If the roll is still locked, unlock it before the next red glow. [Controls](/guides/parry-guard-dodge-controls-guide/).
- **Koo.** Open crowds with Koo. Put Emma back on the stick for the parry string. [When to switch](/guides/beast-of-reincarnation-when-to-use-koo/).
- **Campsite.** The page for that boss names the last fire. Use it. A death run from the Walker door is not practice.

Hasten to the Capital can skip regions. Do not use it on a first clear if you still want the Nushi achievements. [Skip rules](/guides/beast-of-reincarnation-hasten-to-the-capital/).
