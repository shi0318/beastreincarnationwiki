The official Steam store page confirms that *Beast of Reincarnation* has 46 achievements, but it does not describe a New Game Plus mode. A Steam Community achievement walkthrough by Menace123 is marked **Work In Progress** and explicitly separates **Playthrough 1**, **Playthrough 2 - New game plus (NG+)**, and **Post-Playthrough 2 - Farming**. That is useful player evidence, not an official feature specification, so this guide keeps the boundary visible.

## The player-reported NG+ outline

The player guide says its completion plan uses a second playthrough and then farming to reduce the pressure of collectibles, Amber, experience, skills, and weapon upgrades. It also says the author currently believes nothing is missable. Because the guide itself is still WiP, treat those points as a planning hypothesis:

- Back up your save before starting any second run.
- Record which achievements, seedlings, manuals, blades, and map objects you already have.
- Do not assume a New Game Plus transfer list until your own version shows what carries over.
- Use the [interactive map](/guides/interactive-map-guide/) for cleanup rather than deleting a first-run save.

## What Steam does not state

Steam does not currently publish the NG+ unlock trigger, carry-over inventory, enemy scaling, or a second-run-exclusive reward in the store description or global achievement list. Those are exactly the details that should not be filled with guesses. The [achievement guide](/guides/achievements-guide/) lists the official names without pretending to know hidden conditions.
