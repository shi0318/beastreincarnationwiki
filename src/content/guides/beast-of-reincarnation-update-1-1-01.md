v1.1.01 is the eighth update since the August 3, 2026 launch. On consoles it shows as 1.012 (01.012.000) and brings the build to 1.1.01. It is not another difficulty menu. Very Hard and Nightmare already shipped in 1.1.0.

If Chapter 10 still freezes on a cutscene transition, this is the patch to install before you assume the file is dead. Combat that felt unreadable on hour one is a learning curve, not a missing 1.1.01 slider. Cutscene skip was widened in 1.0.11; 1.1.01 does not replace that note.

## After it installs

- Finish the Chapter 10 transition that froze. If it still hangs, the file is the problem, not the old 1.1.0 build.
- Recheck Very Hard and Nightmare on an existing save. Those modes came in 1.1.0. 1.1.01 does not add a third one.
- Skip-cutscene still lives on 1.0.11 and later. Do not diagnose a story scene you cannot skip as a 1.1.01 regression until that version is on the box.
