Patch **v1.1.01** (9 October 2026) is the eighth update for Beast of Reincarnation – shown as 01.012.000 on consoles. Its headline is a new story option that lets you skip three mid-game zones.

## New features

- **Hasten to the Capital:** after the Great Wall Terraces, choose to bypass Pilgrim's Zone, Matsukawa Colony Zone and Fuji Frontier Zone, or play them normally (Mountain Route). Achievements from those zones cannot be earned on a New Game run if you hasten; they remain available on NG+ via the Mountain Route. Full guide: [Hasten to the Capital](/guides/beast-of-reincarnation-hasten-to-the-capital/).
- **Field of View (FOV)** slider in Options.
- Option to **show or hide the skip prompt** for cutscenes and events.

## Cutscene fixes

- Adjusted line pacing and staging across many cutscenes.
- Subtitles no longer stay on screen after a skip.
- Rapid skipping no longer soft-locks the game.
- Character positions and camera angles no longer look wrong after skipping.

## Gameplay and bug fixes

- Cancelling Entanglement Overdrive no longer toggles lock-on.
- Cancelling a strongbox unlock while using a Bloom Art no longer locks the camera on Koo.
- Koo no longer uses items on staggered enemies.
- The "staggered" (絶) graphic no longer lingers.
- **Manual: Explosive Arrow** from a Keeper Golem can now be obtained in NG+.
- Distributed effect-generation load for better performance; many environment gaps, clipping models and invisible walls fixed; submersion depth and collision adjusted.

## The last patch of this phase

Fictions said the next patch, planned for around **19 October** (date to be confirmed), will be the final one in the current phase of major post-launch updates. Feedback can still be sent through Fictions support.
