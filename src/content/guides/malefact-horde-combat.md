This **Beast of Reincarnation Malefact horde guide** covers the released combat loop for crowded encounters. “Horde” describes a group-fight situation here; it is not a claim that every group belongs to one separate enemy family. The winning habit is to reduce incoming decisions one at a time.

## Establish control before damage

1. **Read the selected target.** The weakness icon beside its HP bar and the `weak` marker in Koo's Bloom Arts menu tell you which art is worth testing.
2. **Keep one threat in front of Emma.** A short reposition is safer than spending the entanglement gauge on several early guards.
3. **Parry ordinary swings.** A correct parry stops the hit, advances the yellow stagger bar and builds the resources used by Emma and Koo.
4. **Dodge red attacks.** Red flashes are not normal parry checks. Move before impact, then counter only if the other enemies are not already attacking.
5. **Use Bind to buy space.** Bind is crowd utility; it gives you time to finish a stagger, open Koo's menu or reach a safer angle.

## Use the yellow bar correctly

The yellow bar below an enemy's HP is stagger, not a second health bar. Successful hits and parries fill it. An ordinary opponent can be stunned and then finished with the execution prompt. A powerful enemy or Nushi instead gives a short **daze** opening, so do not wait for an execution prompt that will not appear.

In a group, an execution removes one target but does not freeze the rest. Check the edges of the arena before committing to the finishing animation.

## Match Koo's art to the displayed weakness

The four elemental effects are poison, acid, flame and lightning. Destructive attacks address shields, while Bind affects movement. Do not choose flame just because a Malefact has plants on its body; choose it when the target UI and Bloom Art menu agree.

One practical interaction in the released combat coverage is **Higanbana** creating burn and the **Rapid Cooling** mod returning an entanglement point when it hits a burned enemy. Treat that as an interaction to use when the current target supports it, not as a universal best build for every horde.

If Koo has full FP, Full Bloom can increase damage and stagger pressure for the cast. Spend it on a matching art when the yellow bar is close to full or when a control opening is more valuable than another basic attack.

## A safe group-fight sequence

- Open from range or a favourable angle when the arena allows it.
- Parry the nearest ordinary attack and use the resource gain to open Koo's slowed-time menu.
- Bind or otherwise separate the target that is preventing movement.
- Push the yellow bar on one enemy until the execution or daze opening is ready.
- Reposition during the animation, then repeat rather than standing still to trade with the full group.

For the input details behind this sequence, use the [parry, guard and dodge guide](/guides/parry-guard-dodge-controls-guide/). For a boss-sized version of the same spacing rule, read the [Rangifer Nushi guide](/guides/nushi-boss-fight/).
