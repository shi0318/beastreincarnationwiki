The October 25 update gives returning Beast of Reincarnation players a reason to visit the Cleanse Walker before rushing into another boss. Growth Boost, outfit changes, and expanded New Game+ carryover all affect how a preparation session should work. The useful question is not whether every new option must be used immediately. It is which action improves your next expedition without making you repeat a region you intended to leave behind. This route is a planning recommendation for the updated game, rather than a promise of a particular farming rate or a hidden reward.

## Start with the next expedition's purpose
Before opening the Grow Chamber, decide what the next trip is supposed to accomplish. A short enemy-clearing trip, a regional exploration sweep, and a serious boss attempt ask different things of your supplies. Write down the next destination and the main reason you are going there. If you are still learning a boss, preparation should support repeated attempts; if you are collecting overlooked discoveries, preparation should support time away from camp. Treating every trip as a final boss push encourages unnecessary spending and makes it harder to notice which resource actually ran out.

Check the current equipment and inventory before changing anything. An upgrade material that looks spare may belong to the weapon you are already using. A seed that looks unimportant may be the only seed available to the automatic planting system. Keep the preparation screen open long enough to understand what is present rather than assuming the previous session ended with a full supply. This simple inventory pass is particularly useful after a long break, when memory of a build is usually more confident than accurate.

## Use Growth Boost where it solves a real delay
Growth Boost is an addition to the Grow Chamber. Enemy defeats grant uses, and spending those uses speeds crop growth. That does not establish a universal number of kills per use, a fixed growth-time reduction, or a best crop for every build. Read the current chamber display, identify a crop you actually intend to use, and apply a boost only after understanding the displayed result. There is little practical value in accelerating something that will remain unused while your next trip fails for a different reason.

A sensible loop is to inspect the chamber, complete an expedition that already advances your objective, and inspect it again when you return. This lets ordinary exploration supply boost opportunities instead of turning the entire evening into a separate grind. If you do choose to farm, first record the chamber state and your boost count, then repeat a short route and compare. Stop if the travel and loading cost outweighs the benefit. A route that works for your current position is more useful than an impressive claim with no clear starting point.

## Check Auto planting instead of trusting the label
The update improves Auto behavior so that at least one crop seed is planted when you own seeds. The qualification matters: the setting does not create seeds, and it does not mean every available planting slot will always contain your preferred crop. Check both seed ownership and the chamber result. If the chamber looks inactive, inspect those two conditions before treating the situation as a progression bug. A visual check after returning from an expedition takes less time than leaving for another long trip on an incorrect assumption.

Keep your decisions easy to review. When experimenting with planting behavior, alter one choice, leave the menu normally, and reopen it to confirm what the game retained. If your supplies are limited, avoid testing several unfamiliar options at once. You want to know whether Auto planted, whether Growth Boost changed the intended crop, and whether the next expedition consumed the resources you expected. Separating those observations gives you a usable routine instead of a vague impression that the chamber is sometimes helpful.

## Collect outfits without confusing appearance with power
Emma can now change outfits at Campsites and through the Bed Menu in the Cleanse Walker. Outfit pattern variations are placed across regions and become available after acquisition. That creates a reason to revisit places you left unexplored, but it does not establish a damage bonus or a combat requirement for wearing a particular variation. Choose an appearance you enjoy, confirm that it appears in the relevant menu, and continue using equipment for the combat properties its own description provides.

For an exploration sweep, divide a region into manageable sections and note which paths you have checked. Do not rely solely on the impression that a corridor looks familiar. Check side passages while you are already nearby, then return to camp and inspect newly acquired options. Avoid promising yourself that every unexplored corner contains an outfit: regional placement does not mean a reward at every dead end. The aim is to combine ordinary discovery with collection, reducing repeated travel while preserving the pleasure of finding things yourself.

## Prepare the current save before starting New Game+
The October update adds support for carrying equipment, outfits, and skins into New Game+. Those categories are useful planning facts, but they are not a complete inventory of everything that transfers or resets. Before committing, read the game's current transition prompt and inspect the save you intend to continue. Finish any activity you personally want to complete in the present run. A carryover improvement is not a reason to hurry past unfinished exploration, nor proof that every quest flag and consumable will follow you.

Make a short pre-transition record: equipped weapon, secondary equipment, collected appearances, and unfinished objectives you still care about. If your platform supports multiple save slots through the game's normal interface, understand which slot is being used. Do not manipulate an unfamiliar save folder merely to follow a checklist. After entering the new cycle, check the categories the update specifically supports and confirm your preferred appearance. This turns the transition into something you can inspect, rather than a memory test conducted after the old route has already changed.

## Finish with a useful camp routine
A good return to camp has a clear order: assess the next objective, inspect supplies, review crops, spend boosts deliberately, confirm the appearance you want, and leave with a purpose. If a menu behaves unexpectedly, close and reopen it normally before repeating inputs. The same update includes a fix for a bed-menu soft lock under particular input conditions; installing the current build should come before repeatedly reproducing an old menu problem. Do not treat a patch note as permission to force-close during a save.

The most valuable result is a routine you can repeat without consulting a spreadsheet after every fight. Keep only the notes that change a decision: whether you need more seeds, whether a crop is worth accelerating, which exploration area remains, and whether the current save is ready for another cycle. Growth Boost supports the trip you wanted to take, outfits reward attention to the world, and carryover makes collecting feel less temporary. Used together, they make preparation purposeful instead of extending the time between interesting fights.
