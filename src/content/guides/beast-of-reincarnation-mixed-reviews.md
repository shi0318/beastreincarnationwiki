**Beast of Reincarnation** is **Mixed** on Steam: **1,379** of **2,106** reviews are positive. That is about **65%**. The game launched **3–4 August 2026**.

## What the split is actually about

Recent reviews still recommend the combat, especially on the highest difficulty, and compare it to Sekiro, Black Myth: Wukong, Stellar Blade, or Nier: Automata. The same threads call the loop repetitive, the plot slow, cutscene characters flat, and the launch price too high. Several reports are simple: it keeps crashing.

## Buy, wait, or sale

If you want a long parry-and-dog action RPG and can wait for a discount, that matches the current review language. If you need a tight story or a crash-free first weekend, wait. The Mixed score is not a hidden Very Positive — it is a real split.
