Beast of Reincarnation has been **Mixed** on Steam since launch. That is a real split, not a review bomb: the same reviews often praise the combat and criticise everything around it.

## The numbers

On 15 October 2026 Steam shows **1,424 positive of 2,166 English reviews (Mixed, 66%)** and **3,767 of 6,375 across all languages (Mixed, 59%)**. The standard edition is **$59.99**; there are **46 achievements**.

## Why it is split

| Praise | Criticism |
|---|---|
| Parry combat compared to Sekiro, Stellar Blade and Black Myth: Wukong | Repetitive "explore, camp, repeat" loop in the middle chapters |
| Koo and the Bloom Arts | Slow plot and flat cutscene characters |
| Distinctive flower-and-machine creature design | Bosses that are either simple or huge with off-camera attacks |
| Higher difficulties feel great | Launch price of $59.99 and early crash reports |

## How patches responded

- **Cutscenes:** v1.0.9 to v1.0.12 made almost every scene skippable.
- **Difficulty:** v1.1.0 added Very Hard and Ruthless and raised bosses that were too easy.
- **Length:** v1.1.01 added Hasten to the Capital to skip three zones.
- **Performance:** DLSS 4.5, FSR 4, frame generation and ultrawide support.

## Buy, wait, or sale

If you want a parry-and-companion action RPG and you enjoy higher difficulties, the Mixed score undersells the combat. If you need a fast, tight story, wait for a sale.
