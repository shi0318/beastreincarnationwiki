Hasten to the Capital is a story shortcut after the Great Wall Terraces. It can bypass Pilgrim's Zone, Matsukawa Colony Zone, and Fuji Frontier Zone. On a New Game file, achievements from those zones cannot be earned if you take it. They can still be earned on New Game Plus if you take the Mountain Route. A test hour does not need that fork. A test hour that takes it anyway burns a file you might have wanted to keep.

On **October 26, 2026** the US page was **$59.99**, Mixed, **1,585** English reviews, **67%** of **101** in 30 days. People hasten because the game feels long and the score is uneasy. Length is a real complaint. The skip is the studio's answer for a story run. It is a bad answer for "I just wanted to see if the parry works."

## What the test actually needs

v1.1.01 or newer. Field of View pulled back. One ordinary enemy. One parry. One red-attack dodge. Stop. That is enough to know if the frame and the timing are for you. It does not require the Great Wall Terraces, and it does not require the prompt that appears after them.

If you are already at that prompt because the borrowed evening ran long, read it as a lock, not as a convenience. Pilgrim's Zone holds two Taurus fights, campsites, shrines, and the Sunrise Sword. Matsukawa holds documents, the Resonance Sword, and Hazebringer. Fuji Frontier holds Keeper Golems, the Shark Cutter, and the Explosive Arrow manual. Hastening does not mail those items to the Capital. It leaves them.

The Explosive Arrow fix in the same patch only helps a New Game Plus run that walks back to the Golem. It does not help a New Game file that skipped the zone. If the test file is the only file you plan to keep, take the Mountain Route or stop before the prompt. Do not "just see the Capital" and expect a reload to restore the pops. The notes say they cannot be earned on that New Game run.

## Who the skip is for

New Game Plus players who already have the zone achievements. Story-first players who have decided they do not want those zones or those pops. Both are in the patch note. A Mixed-score shopper on hour one is not.

Erymanth, around level 47, wants the explosive tool those Golems hold. Kunai and Kirin sit higher still. Skipping three chapters and then meeting those numbers is how a shortcut becomes a wall. A test does not need that wall. A kept file might.

## A rule for the uneasy page

The recent 101 reviews are too small to plan a route. The **$59.99** button is not a timer. You can close the game before the terraces and lose nothing but an hour. You cannot un-hasten a New Game file because the month felt long.

Family Sharing is the right container for the test. When the hour works, buy it on the account that will keep the save, and start that save without the skip until you know you want the story fork. When the hour fails, you did not need three zones to learn that.

The major update note said the frequent patch cycle was ending. A route choice now is a choice on this build. Do not hasten because you expect a later patch to return the Sunrise Sword. Walk, or don't start the fork. One parry is the test. The Capital is a different evening.

The October 26 page was $59.99, Mixed, 1,585 English reviews, 67% of 101 in 30 days. That small month is a reason to stop after one parry, not a reason to skip three zones. Pilgrim's Zone, Matsukawa, and Fuji Frontier hold Taurus, swords, documents, Keeper Golems, and the Explosive Arrow manual. Hastening leaves them. New Game Plus can walk the Mountain Route later. A test file that hastens cannot unburn those pops. Family Sharing can host the hour. When the hour works, start the kept save without the skip until you know you want the story fork. When it fails, you did not need the Capital to learn that. The closing patch cycle will not mail the Sunrise Sword back.
