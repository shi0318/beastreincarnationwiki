Steam's news feed still surfaces **Beast of Reincarnation PC Patch Notes: v1.1.01** as the current PC note. Later posts talk about support winding down. They do not replace that patch as the build you should be on before you file a crash.

v1.1.01 added **Hasten to the Capital**, an FOV slider, a skip-prompt toggle, and cutscene and Koo fixes. It did not publish a new difficulty above the modes already in the game.

## Retest on this build

1. Let Steam finish v1.1.01 and restart.
2. Load the save that froze or crashed. The [Chapter 10 freeze](/guides/beast-of-reincarnation-chapter-10-freeze/) page is the hang players still name.
3. If the same step fails, use the [crash checklist](/guides/beast-of-reincarnation-crashes/) before you assume the note missed your GPU.

Support winding down means fewer large patches, not a license to stay on 1.0.7.
