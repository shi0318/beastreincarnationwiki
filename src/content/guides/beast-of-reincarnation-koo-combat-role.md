**Koo** is Emma's blighted dog – and in combat he is half your damage. Emma parries; Koo spends the Flow those parries build. Players who treat him as a mount or a side summon are playing a harder game than they need to.

## What Koo does

| Tool | Role |
|---|---|
| Commands (Pounce, Guard, Incantation) | Engage, protect and support on your call |
| **Bloom Arts** | Elemental and status skills – Higanbana (fire), Sakura: Javelin (shock), Dokuzeri, Kiku, Erica, Numasugi |
| **Nushi skills** | Learned from defeated Nushi – Rangifer's restraint, Charging Botan (Taurus), Dokuzeri Shower (Lacerta) |
| Items | Antidote, Recovery Tonic, Bottle of Acid, Bolt Shards |

## How to use him

1. **Thin packs first.** Send Koo at the small enemies on the edge of a group before Emma commits to the big one.
2. **Spend Flow on knockdowns.** Save Nushi skills for staggered or restrained bosses (Ursa, Shidou).
3. **Match the element.** Koo carries the weakness tool when Emma's blade cannot – Bolt Shards on Lacerta, Bottle of Acid on Taurus and Ursa.
4. **Restrain flyers.** Rangifer's restraint pulls Corvus out of the air.
5. **Give him an Antidote** for poison fights (Lacerta).

## Progression

**Giant Tree in Bloom** tracks all Koo skills; **Floral Profusion** tracks fully upgraded flower techniques. Since v1.0.11 you can reset skills on the flower nodes of the tree. The v1.1.01 patch fixed Koo using items on staggered enemies.
