Koo is the wolf. In a fight he is the reason a pack does not all arrive on Emma at once. He is not a summon you save for the last 20 percent of a boss.

## How to use him

- Send him at the thin enemy on the edge of a group before you commit Emma to the big one. The red-eyed packs in the forest ruins are built for this. If you parry the leader while three small ones swing, the parry does not matter.
- Call him back when a boss does an area blast. A downed wolf is a dead opener for the next phase.
- Do not treat his skill tree as optional flavor. Bloom Arts that extend his engage time are worth more on Nightmare than another point of Emma's sword damage, because the thing that kills runs is being surrounded.

Story scenes with Koo are the ones 1.0.11 lets you skip on a replay. The combat role does not skip. If you only use him as a mount between camps, you are playing a harder game than the one that shipped.
