Amber is the resource players use to fund technique and progression decisions in **Beast of Reincarnation**. The reliable approach is to farm it while making progress through enemies, campsites and optional routes rather than repeating an unverified “best hourly” loop.

## The safe farming loop

1. Start from an activated campsite.
2. Clear ordinary enemies whose attacks you can read and parry.
3. Pick up visible Amber and materials on the same route.
4. Return before your healing, FP options or ammunition are empty.
5. Rest, spend the resource, and repeat only if the route remains efficient.

Parries are valuable because they create the resources needed for Koo's Bloom Arts and Emma's combat tools while you farm. A route that teaches timing and produces upgrade materials is usually more useful than a route that only produces a larger unverified number.

## What to spend first

Early Amber is best reserved for the movement, Bloom Art and blade-art priorities described in the [first-hours build guide](/guides/first-hours-build-and-combat/). The [Amber technique points page](/guides/amber-technique-points-farm/) contains the current focused discussion.

Do not spend every point on a damage branch before checking whether the next Cleanse Walker level or traversal upgrade is blocking exploration. The [Walker materials guide](/guides/cleanse-walker-upgrade-materials-guide/) explains that separate gate.

## Rate and patch warning

Enemy density, drops and route safety can change with updates or difficulty. No universal Amber-per-minute number is claimed here. Record the region, difficulty, patch, route time and deaths if you want to compare a farm honestly.
