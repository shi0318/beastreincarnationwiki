**Shidou** is a fast, samurai-style fighter and – with Kunai – one of Emma's two rivals. He fights alongside **Nue**, a lightning-summoning Nushi, and the pair's final meeting on **Mount Hiei** is one of the toughest fights of the late game.

## How he fights

- Two-to-four-hit sword combos and a two-swing branch attack – all parryable.
- A vertical tree slam that cannot be parried – perfect-dodge the landing, then counter.
- He **parries ranged attacks**, so arrows are wasted on him.
- At low health he **siphons Nue's health** – use it: bring him to about 30%, let him drain Nue, repeat, then finish him.

Patch v1.1.0 (1 October 2026) updated some of Shidou's attack patterns, with further changes planned.
