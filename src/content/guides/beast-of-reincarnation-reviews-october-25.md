On **October 25, 2026**, the US Steam page for Beast of Reincarnation listed the game at **$59.99** with no discount shown. English reviews on that page were **Mixed**. The page counted **1,585** reviews for the game, and **68%** of **109** reviews in the last 30 days were positive.

That is a store-page English snapshot. It is not the same filter as an all-language total from earlier in the week. Do not average them. Do not say the score "fell" from a larger all-language count to 1,585. The 1,585 is the English line the page printed today. The 109 is the recent English slice. Both are small enough to read carefully, and large enough to stop you buying on a memory of launch week.

The game has been out since **August 4, 2026**. A Mixed label this late is not a day-one pile-on. It is the label still sitting on the buy button.

## How to read 1,585 and 109

**1,585** English reviews is the all-time line on that page. **109** in 30 days, at **68%** positive, is the recent line. One hundred and nine reviews can swing a percentage. Sixty-eight percent of 109 is not a new patch verdict. It is a month of English players who bothered to review. A single heated thread can move a sample that size. Treat it as a warning light, not as a balance sheet.

The Mixed label on the all-time English line is the stabler fact. It matches the argument that has been on the page for weeks: some long files like the combat and the dog, some long files reject the story, and the camera was a real launch complaint that a later patch put a slider on. Today's page does not erase that split. It prices it at **$59.99**.

If you already played it on a shared library or on Game Pass, you do not need this percentage. You need the field-of-view slider and one fight. If you have not played it, the percentage is a reason to borrow it before you buy a second copy, not a reason to write a thesis.

## What the button is selling

Single-player. Achievements. Full controller support. Steam Cloud. Family Sharing. Adjustable difficulty. Those boxes were on the store page before today, and the October 25 price did not add co-op to them. A shared library is two people taking turns. It is not two people in the same fight.

v1.1.01 is still the patch that matters for the camera. It added Field of View and a skip-prompt toggle. It also added Hasten to the Capital after the Great Wall Terraces, with an achievement cost on a New Game file. None of that is a discount. None of that flips Mixed to Very Positive by itself. Install it before you judge a review written in August.

Story, Normal, and Hard are damage settings. The slider is the camera. Do not drop to Story because the frame is close. Widen the frame, parry one beast, then decide if Normal hurts too much. [That split](/guides/beast-of-reincarnation-story-mode-is-not-the-camera/) is still the first menu.

## Who should pay $59.99 today

Pay if you already know you want Emma and Koo on your own account, and a Mixed English page does not scare you off a parry game. The recent 68% does not prove the game got worse this month. It proves the people reviewing in English this month are not a cheer squad. Read that and still buy only if the loop is the thing you came for.

Borrow if the open question is the camera or the first hour. Family Sharing and Cloud are on the feature list. One evening on a borrowed copy answers more than 109 reviews. Do not start a long boss on a borrow if the owner needs the library back.

Wait if you need a sale. The October 25 US page did not show one. Waiting for a discount is a store decision. Waiting for the Mixed label to become Very Positive is not a plan this page supports.

Skip if the story is the only reason you play, and you already believe the long negative files. Today's count does not rehabilitate a story those players rejected. It also does not prove them right. It prices the argument at full fare.

2. Set field of view. Fight one ordinary enemy on Normal.
3. Do not hasten to the Capital on a file you might keep. That skip blocks New Game achievements in three zones.
4. If the hour works, buy it on the account that will keep the save. If it does not, you saved **$59.99**.
