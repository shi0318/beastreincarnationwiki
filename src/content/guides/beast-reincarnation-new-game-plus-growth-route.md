**New Game Plus** is most useful when you decide what the second run is for before leaving the first one. The October 25 update adds carryover for equipment, outfits, and skins; it also changes enemy and boss behavior and adds a Grow Chamber boost. Those facts make a replay more flexible, but they do not establish a universal carryover rule for every quest, collectible, or achievement. Use this route to organize what is confirmed and keep the uncertain categories visible.

## Pick one purpose for the replay

Choose one of three goals: finish the story faster, revisit regions for missed content, or practice combat with a different build. A fast story run should prioritize the main path and use any available progression shortcut only after checking which zone achievements it may skip. A cleanup run should keep a regional checklist and avoid bypassing locations until you have confirmed what remains. A combat-practice run should use a consistent weapon, skill set, and difficulty for several encounters so you can judge improvement rather than random variation.

Write the goal down before you start. This sounds simple, but it prevents a common NG+ problem: turning every detour into a completion task and losing the reason the run was meant to be shorter. If the goal changes, update the note instead of pretending the old route still applies.

## Make a carryover inventory before the reset

Before choosing New Game Plus, make a short list of equipment, outfits, and skins you want to see carried over. The patch names those categories specifically. Capture your current weapon and equipment setup, note any cosmetic pattern you found, and compare the new file after it starts. Do not infer that Spirit Stones, materials, quest progress, region completion, or every achievement will behave identically. The update announcement does not promise that.

If you care about any other category, verify it with a low-risk test or consult the game's own current menu. Avoid using community save editors to force an answer: they can change the very file you are trying to inspect. Keep a backup if your platform supports it and verify cloud synchronization before experimenting. The [save safety guide](/guides/beast-of-reincarnation-save-safety-guide/) explains why a cloud icon alone is not a complete recovery plan.

## Turn exploration into a repeatable loop

Use a simple regional loop: open the map, list the campsites and activities you still need, and choose one route that ends near a safe return point. Collect outfit-pattern variations as you explore, but treat them as appearance unlocks unless the game shows a gameplay effect. Do not cross an entire region solely because you expect a pattern to be at an unverified location. Mark observed pickups with the build and route so future attempts are reproducible.

The Cleanse Walker is a useful reset point for the next leg. Check Emma's bed menu for outfit options, inspect the Grow Chamber, and confirm your current equipment before departing. When Auto is enabled and you have seeds, the updated chamber guarantees at least one seed is planted. That is helpful for a longer exploration stretch, but it is still worth checking the plot instead of assuming a harvest is ready.

## Spend Growth Boost when the timing matters

Defeating enemies grants Growth Boost uses that speed up crop growth. The boost matters most when the crop will unlock a needed item, when you have enough time before the next major fight, or when you are already clearing enemies on the route. It matters less when a crop is almost ready or when you will return to the Walker shortly. Use one boost, inspect the result, and then decide whether the next one is worth it. This avoids spending every use without knowing its practical value in your current run.

Do not confuse faster crop growth with guaranteed recipe output or a full chamber. The patch changed the planting behavior under Auto, not the entire crafting economy. Keep the crop, seed count, and elapsed route notes separate if you are comparing runs.

## Relearn combat in small tests

The update adds boss attacks and adjusts some patterns, enemy attributes, Nushi stats, and selected Blade Arts and Bloom Arts. Start a replay fight by watching one cycle without trying to punish every opening. Name the move you saw, observe whether it tracks, and test a single answer. The held guard input is especially worth practicing in a low-stakes encounter because tapping it from muscle memory may not match the new behavior.

Ranged weapons can be changed during regular attacks and Iaido stance, which gives you more options when a target backs away. Use this as a deliberate transition: one combo, then a range check, then a switch only if the opening supports it. If you alter a skill, weapon, and difficulty simultaneously, your notes will not tell you why the fight changed. For a clean comparison, change one variable at a time.

## Do not lose optional content to a shortcut

An earlier progression option can bypass several zones after the Great Wall Terraces. That can help a story-focused replay, but a skipped zone may close off achievements until a later run. Before taking the shortcut, check your own regional checklist and the [chapter list](/guides/beast-of-reincarnation-13-chapters/). If you are unsure whether a collectible or quest is complete, take the longer route; a few minutes of verification is cheaper than restarting the whole loop.

## End the session with useful notes

After each session, record the last campsite, the zone objective, the equipment you used, the Growth Boost state, and any boss move that felt different. Separate observed facts from guesses. A note such as “guard held through two light hits on v1.1.02” is more useful than “guard is stronger now.” The same discipline keeps outfit patterns, crop timing, and achievement cleanup from blending together.

New Game Plus works best as a focused second journey. Carry the gear and cosmetics the update explicitly supports, use the chamber when timing makes sense, and let changed enemy behavior teach you the new rhythm instead of forcing your first-run script.
