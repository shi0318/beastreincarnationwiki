**Rangifer** is the Chapter 1 Nushi in the Ogouchi Buffer Zone, and you fight it twice. The crater is one health bar, around level 9. The sealed lair near Subcity 03 is two bars, around level 12, and it adds a jump. Steam lists **Rangifer's End** at 53.5% of owners. The full record is the [Rangifer page](/bosses/rangifer/).

Fire is the weakness. On the rematch, Burn resists, so use raw fire damage rather than a burn proc you already saw fail.

## Crater clear

1. Light the Cliff-Facing Tower campsite before you drop. That is the retry point.
2. Extend to the high ground and open with an air assassination. Do not run in on the flat.
3. Stand slightly back. Parry a normal antler swing and hit the lowered head. Each parry fills Flow for Koo.
4. When the body glows red, leave. The four-sweep is not a parry string. See [red attacks](/guides/beast-of-reincarnation-red-attacks/).

Go into the lair at level 10 or higher, with a fire blade already equipped. Swapping after the first bar is how the rematch snowballs. The [weakness chart](/guides/beast-of-reincarnation-boss-weakness-chart/) is the loadout, not a damage calculator.
