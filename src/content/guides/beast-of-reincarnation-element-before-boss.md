The [boss weakness chart](/guides/beast-of-reincarnation-boss-weakness-chart/) is a loadout page. Read it at the Cleanse Walker, swap the blade, then enter. Swapping after the first phase wastes the opening parries.

The published split is short. **Rangifer** wants fire. **Lacerta** wants shock. **Taurus** and **Ursa** want acid. **Erymanth** wants explosive. The duo fights, Kunai with Kirin and Shidou with Nue, are the poison pair. The final fight is the exception: no elemental weakness, and it opens behind a regenerating Nanite Shield.

## A Walker checklist

1. Open the chart, not a tier list. Blade passives are Shock, Poison, Acid, Flame, and healing. Pick the one the chart names.
2. If the blade is still on a previous region, buy the merchant copy from Brad when the page says that sword returns to his shop. Do not hunt a chest you already missed if the shop row exists.
3. Poison on a duo is for both health bars. Do not save it for the second name and then eat the first.

HP and frame data are not on the chart. Do not add them. The element is the decision this page is for.
