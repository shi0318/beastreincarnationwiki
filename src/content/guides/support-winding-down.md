Steam's news feed currently leads with Game Freak winding down active support for **Beast of Reincarnation**. That is a support headline, not a new difficulty and not a discount.

Checked October 19, 2026: English reviews are still **Mixed**, 1,430 positive out of 2,173. The US price is still **$59.99**, discount 0. Forty-six achievements. The PC patch notes still top out at **v1.1.01**.

## What to do with that headline

- Install v1.1.01 before you report a Chapter 10 freeze or a crash. [Current patch](/guides/beast-of-reincarnation-patch-1-1-01-still-current/).
- Do not wait on this headline for a sale. The tag has not moved. [Price](/guides/beast-of-reincarnation-price-october-16/).
- A Mixed score with support ending means the combat loop you have is the loop you are buying. [Red attacks](/guides/beast-of-reincarnation-red-attacks/) and [Lacerta](/guides/how-to-beat-lacerta/) are the fights that decide that purchase.

A later small hotfix can still land. Do not treat "winding down" as "the build is frozen today."
