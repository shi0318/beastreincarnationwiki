The official Steam description confirms three broad loadout directions for *Beast of Reincarnation*: **ranged, stealthy, and aggressive**. It also names the systems used to shape those directions: skill trees, gear, and spirit stones. Steam does not publish a complete tier list, final damage table, or one best build, so the useful answer is to map the official choices to a testable playstyle rather than inventing numbers.

## Three source-backed directions

### Ranged

Choose ranged when you want distance, projectile testing, and a safer opening before committing Emma to a melee exchange. Track the arrow and bolt manuals separately; Steam's **Manual Maniac** achievement explicitly asks for every manual. The [achievement checklist](/guides/achievements-guide/) records that objective.

### Stealthy

Choose stealthy when you prefer route planning, positioning, and avoiding unnecessary groups. The official page names stealth as a supported loadout direction, but it does not publish a universal detection meter, damage multiplier, or guaranteed assassination rule. Test each claim in the released build.

### Aggressive

Choose aggressive when you want Emma's direct sword abilities and Koo's commands to stay in the fight. That pairs naturally with the [parry and controls guide](/guides/parry-guard-dodge-controls-guide/) and the [Bloom Arts guide](/guides/koo-bloom-arts-qte-guide/), both of which document shipped combat systems.

## How to compare a build honestly

Change one skill, gear piece, or spirit stone at a time. Record the enemy, difficulty, attack used, and whether Koo's technique was active. A player impression is useful, but it is not a universal tier list. Until a full stat table is published and checked, “best build” should mean best for a stated playstyle and encounter, not an unsupported ranking.
