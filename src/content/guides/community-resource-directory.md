This directory records what the authors say their resources cover, keeps their links intact, and does not copy routes or mark unverified locations as confirmed. It was checked on **2026-08-08**.

Use it as a starting point when a guide on this site does not yet cover your task. Check each author's update date and screenshots before relying on a collectible location, a percentage claim, or a configuration edit after a game patch.

## How to use a map without losing your own progress

1. Open the map only after you have recorded your current in-game objective and region.
2. Use one filter or one target at a time; do not clear an entire region from an unlabeled collection of pins.
3. Match a marker with an in-game landmark, checkpoint, or screenshot before travelling far from your route.
4. If a map entry is missing or wrong, report it to that map's author instead of treating it as a fact about the shipped game.
5. Keep your own notes for collectibles that affect your build, because community markers and version numbers can change.

## Treat configuration advice as an experiment

Community settings advice can be valuable, especially when it tells you exactly which option changed the image for that author. It still needs a controlled check on your own PC:

- Change one setting at a time and return to the same scene.
- Record the resolution, upscaler, display mode, and frame-rate cap before comparing results.
- Back up any configuration file before editing it.
- Undo an edit if it causes crashes, visual artifacts, or a new problem after an update.

The separate [PC performance and settings guide](/guides/pc-performance-settings-fixes/) identifies its player reports and official statements in more detail. Use the [official PC requirements page](/guides/pc-system-requirements/) for the hardware targets published by the developer, not a community post.
