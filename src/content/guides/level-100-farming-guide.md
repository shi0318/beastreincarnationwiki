## The useful early farm with a published yield

The Steam Community route by Mr.White reports **3 Technique Points and 150-180 Amber in roughly 10 seconds** near an Ogouchi Buffer Zone campsite. That is a player report, not a guaranteed universal rate. The full [Amber and Technique Point farm](/guides/amber-technique-points-farm/) records the map position, reset assumptions, and the author's screenshots.

To make the number meaningful for your save:

1. Record Amber and Technique Points before ten repetitions.
2. Use the same campsite and enemy group.
3. Time the whole loop, including reset and movement, not just the final attacks.
4. Compare your result with the reported 3 / 150-180 / roughly 10-second figure.

## What is not yet a verified level-100 route

The available Steam sources do not publish a complete XP table, an official level cap, or a guaranteed endgame farming loop. Do not quote a specific XP-per-minute number from this page. If your goal is a level target, prioritize survivability and upgrade gates first; the [Cleanse Walker materials guide](/guides/cleanse-walker-upgrade-materials-guide/) explains why equipment progression can require exploration rather than pure combat farming.
